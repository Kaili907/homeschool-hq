## Safety — read this before you touch anything

### What could go wrong, and what stops it

- **Physical hazard.** Looking at the sun causes permanent eye damage.
  - What stops it: NEVER look at the sun directly, through any lens, grating, CD, camera, or filter. All solar work is done by measuring warming water in shade-versus-sun, with the learner's back to the sun.
- **Physical hazard.** Working outdoors in sun risks heat and sunburn, and a sealed dark container left in full sun pressurises.
  - What stops it: Leave the water container OPEN - never capped or sealed - in the sun. Limit exposure to 30 minutes at a time, wear a hat and sunscreen, keep drinking water available, and work in the cooler part of the day.

### Do these steps in this order — do not reorder them

1. The published-data route is the DEFAULT and needs no outdoor work at all.
2. If the outdoor route runs, the learner works with their back to the sun and never looks toward it - not directly and not through a lens, filter, grating, CD, or camera.
3. Wear a hat and sunscreen, take drinking water, and work in the cooler part of the day, for no more than 30 minutes at a time.
4. Leave the dark water container OPEN in the sun; it is never capped or sealed.
5. Read the thermometer at fixed intervals from the shade, and come indoors at the scheduled end or at the first sign of overheating.

### Stop at once if any of these happens

- Stop instantly if anyone looks toward the sun through any optical device.
- Stop and move indoors at any sign of overheating.
- Stop for any injury, burn, spill, fume, or allergic reaction and tell the supervising adult.
- Stop if a material, tool, or step is not the one this lesson specifies.
- Burn: cool it under running cool water for 20 minutes. Do not use ice, butter, or ointment.
- Splash in an eye: rinse with running water for 15 minutes, holding the eyelid open, before anything else.
- Fumes or a strong smell: leave the room, open a window from outside the room, and do not go back in to tidy up.
- Fire: do not use water. Get everyone out, close the door, and call the emergency number. Smother a very small contained flame with a metal pan lid or a fire blanket only if that is safe to do without reaching over it.
- If anyone may have swallowed a magnet, a battery, or any material from a lesson, treat it as an emergency and seek medical help at once. Do not wait for symptoms.
