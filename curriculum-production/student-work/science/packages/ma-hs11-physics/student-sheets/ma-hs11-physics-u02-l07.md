### What could go wrong, and what stops it

- **Physical hazard.** Falling weights can land on feet or break flooring.
  - What stops it: Keep the hanging mass under 200 g, place a cushion or towel below the drop zone, keep feet clear, and never stand under the hanging mass.
- **Physical hazard.** String under tension can snap back toward the face.
  - What stops it: Wear eye protection whenever the string is under tension, use light loads only, inspect the string along its whole length before each run, and stand to the side of the string line rather than in it.

### Do these steps in this order — do not reorder them

1. Put a cushion or folded towel under the drop zone and keep every foot clear of it.
2. Put on eye protection before the string is put under any tension.
3. Inspect the string along its whole length before each run and replace it at the first sign of fraying.
4. Start with the lightest load and add mass, up to 200 g, only after a clean run.
5. Stand to the side of the string line, never in it, and never under the hanging mass.
