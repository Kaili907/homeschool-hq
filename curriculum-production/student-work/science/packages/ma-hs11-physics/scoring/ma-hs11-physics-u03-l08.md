**Fixed for this topic.** These are settled, and work that contradicts one is `Not yet` on Scientific correctness:

- In the absence of a net external force, the total momentum of a system is unchanged by interactions within it.

**Accepted relationships and models.** What this lesson's learning target asserts:

- Total momentum before an interaction equals total momentum after it, provided no net external force acts.
- Momentum conservation holds for every collision type, including those where kinetic energy is not conserved.
- Defining the system is part of applying the principle: a force is external or internal only with respect to a stated system.

**Disqualifying errors.** Each of these is `Not yet` on Scientific correctness however well the reasoning is documented and however complete the evidence is:

- Claiming momentum is conserved only in elastic collisions.
- Applying momentum conservation to a system with a substantial unaccounted external force.
- Adding momenta as scalars and concluding momentum was not conserved.

**Grade boundary.** Centre-of-mass frame analysis and two-dimensional collision algebra are acceptable but not required.
