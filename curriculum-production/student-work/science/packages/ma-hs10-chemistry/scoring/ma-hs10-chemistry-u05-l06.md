**Accepted relationships and models.** What this lesson's learning target asserts:

- A balanced equation is a mathematical statement about relative numbers of particles, and mole ratios are read directly from its coefficients.
- Mass, moles, and particle count are related through molar mass and Avogadro's number, and calculations move between them in a stated order.
- A calculated result carries the units and the precision of the measurements it came from.

**Also correct.** Accept any of these framings — do not require the wording above:

- Dimensional analysis and proportional reasoning both yield correct results and are equally acceptable.

**Disqualifying errors.** Each of these is `Not yet` on Scientific correctness however well the reasoning is documented and however complete the evidence is:

- Reading mole ratios from an unbalanced equation.
- Applying coefficients as mass ratios rather than mole ratios.
- Reporting a calculated result with more significant figures than the input measurements support.

**Grade boundary.** Solution stoichiometry with concentration and gas law stoichiometry are acceptable but not required here.
