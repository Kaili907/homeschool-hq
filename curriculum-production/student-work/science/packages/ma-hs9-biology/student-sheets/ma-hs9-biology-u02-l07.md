### What could go wrong, and what stops it

- **Chemical hazard.** Isopropyl alcohol is flammable, its vapour is heavier than air and pools at bench level, and it is harmful if swallowed or splashed in eyes.
  - What stops it: An adult sweeps the room for every ignition source before the bottle is opened, pours no more than 60 mL, and returns the bottle to a closed cupboard at once. Eye protection on; nothing is tasted. See the safe order.
- **Physical hazard.** Glass containers can break and cut.
  - What stops it: Use plastic or unbreakable cups only.

### Do these steps in this order — do not reorder them

1. An adult sweeps the room before the bottle is opened: hob and oven off, heater off, no candle, pilot light, toaster, charger, or battery anywhere.
2. Put on eye protection. Mash the fruit and add the soap-and-salt water and filter it, all with no alcohol yet in the room.
3. An adult brings in the cold isopropyl, pours no more than 60 mL into the learner's cup, caps the bottle, and returns it to a closed cupboard at once.
4. Observe and spool the DNA with the cup on a tray, at least three metres from any appliance or outlet.
5. Dispose of the alcohol mixture before anything electrical is switched on at that surface again.
