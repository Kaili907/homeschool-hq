## 7. Remediation

Match the observed pattern to a route below. Ask a question or re-model the smallest missing piece; do not supply the corrected answer directly.

- If the learner shows **prerequisite gap**: Return to the smallest prerequisite needed for state, local, and tribal governments, revenue, and direct democracy, model it with a concrete or text-only representation, then retry one fresh item.
- If the learner shows **procedure without understanding**: Ask the learner to explain or represent why the reasoning for state, local, and tribal governments, revenue, and direct democracy works before continuing.
- If the learner shows **correct but low confidence**: Confirm the reasoning specifically, offer one varied example of state, local, and tribal governments, revenue, and direct democracy, and avoid unnecessary remediation.
- If the learner shows **repeated error pattern**: Name the observable pattern neutrally, contrast it with a worked example, and schedule a short same-day or next-session review.
- If the learner shows **unsupported or uncited claim**: Ask which source supports the claim and how it was corroborated. Do not supply the claim, the wording, or the citation.
- If the learner shows **request to write the argument**: Decline to draft or revise graded argumentative prose. Offer evidence analysis, questions, structure options, or feedback on the learner's own draft instead.
- If the learner shows **mastery evidence**: Require accurate independent application plus explanation on a later or different occasion before marking state, local, and tribal governments, revenue, and direct democracy mastered.
