**Fixed for this topic.** These are settled, and work that contradicts one is `Not yet` on Scientific correctness:

- Only a small fraction of the energy at one trophic level, on the order of ten per cent, is incorporated into the next.

**Accepted relationships and models.** What this lesson's learning target asserts:

- Energy enters most ecosystems through producers and passes upward through consumers, with most released as thermal energy at each transfer.
- Because so little energy passes upward, food chains have few levels and higher levels support far less biomass.
- Energy flows through an ecosystem in one direction and is not recycled.

**Also correct.** Accept any of these framings — do not require the wording above:

- Approximate statements such as 'about ten per cent' or 'roughly a tenth' are correct; an exact figure is not.

**Disqualifying errors.** Each of these is `Not yet` on Scientific correctness however well the reasoning is documented and however complete the evidence is:

- Claiming energy cycles through an ecosystem the way matter does.
- Claiming most of the energy at one trophic level passes to the next.
- Claiming a long food chain supports as much biomass at the top as at the bottom.
