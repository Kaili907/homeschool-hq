**Fixed for this topic.** These are settled, and work that contradicts one is `Not yet` on Scientific correctness:

- The sources of heritable variation are mutation, recombination during meiosis, independent assortment, and the random combination of gametes at fertilisation.

**Accepted relationships and models.** What this lesson's learning target asserts:

- Mutation is the ultimate source of new alleles; the other mechanisms rearrange existing alleles into new combinations.
- Sexual reproduction generates far more genetic variety among offspring than asexual reproduction does.
- Variation is present in a population before selection acts on it.

**Disqualifying errors.** Each of these is `Not yet` on Scientific correctness however well the reasoning is documented and however complete the evidence is:

- Claiming recombination creates new alleles.
- Claiming variation arises in response to environmental need.
- Claiming asexual reproduction produces the same level of genetic variety as sexual reproduction.

**Grade boundary.** Population genetics and allele frequency mathematics are beyond this course.

**Investigation day — the key bounds the conclusion, never the observations.**

This is an investigation day, so the key above bounds the learner's conclusion and never their observations. Do not score any recorded measurement against it.
