**Fixed for this topic.** These are settled, and work that contradicts one is `Not yet` on Scientific correctness:

- Weather changes occur at the boundaries between air masses of different temperature and humidity.

**Accepted relationships and models.** What this lesson's learning target asserts:

- An air mass takes on the temperature and humidity of the region it forms over.
- Where air masses of different properties meet, the warmer, less dense air is forced upward, it cools, water vapour condenses, and clouds and precipitation form.
- The kind of weather depends on the properties of the air masses involved and on how they meet.

**Also correct.** Accept any of these framings — do not require the wording above:

- Warm front, cold front, and occluded front are correct terms; a plain description of the interaction is equally acceptable.

**Disqualifying errors.** Each of these is `Not yet` on Scientific correctness however well the reasoning is documented and however complete the evidence is:

- Claiming warm air sinks below cold air.
- Claiming precipitation forms without air rising and cooling.
- Claiming an air mass has the same properties wherever it forms.

**Grade boundary.** Synoptic meteorology, adiabatic lapse rates, and numerical forecasting are High School work.
