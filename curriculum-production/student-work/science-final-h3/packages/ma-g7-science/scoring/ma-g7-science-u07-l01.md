**Fixed for this topic.** These are settled, and work that contradicts one is `Not yet` on Scientific correctness:

- The water cycle is driven by energy from the Sun and by gravity.

**Accepted relationships and models.** What this lesson's learning target asserts:

- Energy from the Sun drives evaporation and transpiration, moving water from surface to atmosphere.
- Gravity drives precipitation, runoff, and the downward movement of water through the ground.
- Water changes state during the cycle but the total amount of water on Earth stays essentially the same.

**Also correct.** Accept any of these framings — do not require the wording above:

- Including transpiration, sublimation, and infiltration among the processes is correct; naming every process is not required.

**Disqualifying errors.** Each of these is `Not yet` on Scientific correctness however well the reasoning is documented and however complete the evidence is:

- Claiming the water cycle creates or destroys water.
- Claiming clouds form because water becomes lighter than air rather than by condensation on particles.
- Claiming the cycle runs without any energy input.

**Grade boundary.** Quantitative water budgets and residence times are High School work.
