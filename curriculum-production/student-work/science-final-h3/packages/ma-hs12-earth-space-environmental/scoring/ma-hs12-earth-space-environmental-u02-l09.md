**Fixed for this topic.** These are settled, and work that contradicts one is `Not yet` on Scientific correctness:

- A star's mass determines its life cycle and its end state: white dwarf for low and intermediate masses, neutron star or black hole for high masses.

**Accepted relationships and models.** What this lesson's learning target asserts:

- Stars form from collapsing clouds of gas and dust, spend most of their lives fusing hydrogen on the main sequence, and then evolve as their core fuel is exhausted.
- Low and intermediate mass stars expand to red giants, shed their outer layers, and leave a white dwarf.
- High-mass stars end in supernova explosions, leaving a neutron star or a black hole.

**Also correct.** Accept any of these framings — do not require the wording above:

- Hertzsprung-Russell diagram reasoning is correct but not required.

**Disqualifying errors.** Each of these is `Not yet` on Scientific correctness however well the reasoning is documented and however complete the evidence is:

- Claiming all stars end their lives as supernovae.
- Claiming a star's life cycle depends principally on its brightness rather than on its mass.
- Claiming stars burn fuel chemically.

**Grade boundary.** Detailed stellar evolution tracks and mass-loss modelling are beyond this course.
