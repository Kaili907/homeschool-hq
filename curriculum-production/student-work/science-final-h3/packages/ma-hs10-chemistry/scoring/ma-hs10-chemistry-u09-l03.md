**Fixed for this topic.** These are settled, and work that contradicts one is `Not yet` on Scientific correctness:

- Fission is the splitting of a heavy nucleus into lighter nuclei, releasing energy and typically neutrons.

**Accepted relationships and models.** What this lesson's learning target asserts:

- Fission of a heavy nucleus releases energy because the products are more tightly bound per nucleon than the original nucleus.
- Released neutrons can induce further fissions, which is the basis of a chain reaction.
- Control of a fission reactor works by controlling the number of neutrons available to induce further fission.

**Disqualifying errors.** Each of these is `Not yet` on Scientific correctness however well the reasoning is documented and however complete the evidence is:

- Claiming fission is the combining of nuclei.
- Claiming fission energy comes from breaking chemical bonds.
- Claiming a reactor's fuel can undergo a nuclear explosion in the manner of a weapon.

**Grade boundary.** Reactor engineering, cross sections, and criticality calculations are beyond this course.
