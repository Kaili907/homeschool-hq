**Fixed for this topic.** These are settled, and work that contradicts one is `Not yet` on Scientific correctness:

- In order of increasing frequency and decreasing wavelength the spectrum runs radio, microwave, infrared, visible, ultraviolet, X-ray, gamma.
- All electromagnetic waves travel at the same speed in vacuum, approximately three times ten to the eighth metres per second.

**Accepted relationships and models.** What this lesson's learning target asserts:

- The regions of the spectrum differ in frequency and wavelength, not in their fundamental nature.
- Photon energy increases with frequency, so gamma rays carry far more energy per photon than radio waves.
- Only the higher-frequency regions, from roughly ultraviolet upward, carry enough energy per photon to ionise atoms.

**Also correct.** Accept any of these framings — do not require the wording above:

- Ordering the spectrum by wavelength or by frequency are equally correct, provided the direction is stated.

**Disqualifying errors.** Each of these is `Not yet` on Scientific correctness however well the reasoning is documented and however complete the evidence is:

- Placing the spectrum regions out of order, such as putting ultraviolet below visible in frequency.
- Claiming different regions of the spectrum travel at different speeds in vacuum.
- Claiming radio waves or microwaves are ionising radiation.

**Grade boundary.** Quantitative photon energy calculation is acceptable but not required.

**Declared hazards — each one also appears in the learner's safety brief:**

- physical: Building a device involves cutting and small parts. — mitigation: An adult performs all cutting with sharp tools, the learner uses blunt scissors or pre-cut parts, and small parts stay away from young children.
- physical: Aluminium foil laid across both terminals of a cell is a short circuit and a standard ignition demonstration. — mitigation: Foil is used for shielding only and never touches both terminals of any cell. The shielding strand runs first and uses no electricity at all.
- physical: Battery-powered builds can short and heat, and a stalled motor draws its full stall current. — mitigation: Single 1.5 V cells only - never mains electricity, never a lithium or button cell. Tape the terminals before and after use, disconnect between tests, disconnect immediately if a motor stalls or will not turn, and stop if anything warms.
- online: Evaluating published radiation claims means reading sources of uncertain quality. — mitigation: An adult approves each source in advance. No account creation, sign-in, purchase, or personal information is required, and the learner never contacts a site owner.

**Guardian visibility note:** Share the lesson target, completion state, evidence type, and next instructional step. For any hands-on lesson, share the hazard list, the supervision level, the stop conditions, the safe order, the required PPE, and the disposal steps - including any step that runs unattended or overnight - BEFORE the session so the guardian can review them in advance. **Safe order the learner is given — read it before the session starts:**

- An adult approves every source before reading begins, and does all cutting with sharp tools.
- Run the shielding strand FIRST; it uses no electricity at all.
- Keep aluminium foil for shielding only - it never touches both terminals of any cell.
- For the energy-conversion build an adult connects one 1.5 V cell, never mains, never a lithium or button cell.
- Run each test for a few seconds, disconnecting between tests, and disconnect at once if a motor stalls or anything warms.
- Disconnect and remove the cell before the build is stored.

**Protective equipment:** Eye protection: not required.

**Disposal and clearing up:** Disconnect and remove the cell and tape its terminals; used cells go to household battery recycling in a labelled container, never the general bin. Sweep up off-cuts and small parts and bin them away from young children. Recycle the foil and cardboard. Nothing is left connected or stored with a cell in it.

**Stop conditions on record, adult wording:**

- Stop if any component becomes warm.
- Stop if a source requests an account, payment, or personal information.
- Never connect any build to a wall outlet.
- Stop for any injury, burn, spill, fume, or allergic reaction and tell the supervising adult.
- Stop if a material, tool, or step is not the one this lesson specifies.
- Burn: cool it under running cool water for 20 minutes. Do not use ice, butter, or ointment.
