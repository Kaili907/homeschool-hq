**Fixed for this topic.** These are settled, and work that contradicts one is `Not yet` on Scientific correctness:

- Kinetic energy depends on mass and on the square of the speed; gravitational potential energy near Earth's surface depends on mass, gravitational field strength, and height above a chosen reference.

**Accepted relationships and models.** What this lesson's learning target asserts:

- Doubling an object's speed quadruples its kinetic energy, which is why stopping distance grows so steeply with speed.
- Potential energy is defined relative to a chosen reference level, and only differences in it are physically meaningful.
- Potential energy belongs to a system of interacting objects, such as an object and Earth, not to one object alone.

**Disqualifying errors.** Each of these is `Not yet` on Scientific correctness however well the reasoning is documented and however complete the evidence is:

- Claiming kinetic energy is proportional to speed rather than to its square.
- Reporting a potential energy without stating the reference level.
- Claiming potential energy is stored inside a single object independently of any interaction.

**Grade boundary.** Elastic potential energy formulas and relativistic kinetic energy are acceptable but not required.

**Investigation day — the key bounds the conclusion, never the observations.**
