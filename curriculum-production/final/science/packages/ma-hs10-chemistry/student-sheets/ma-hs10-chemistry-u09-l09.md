## The science information and exact work for this lesson

Everything required below is printed on this sheet. You do not need an unseen case, model, table, prior answer, or outside source.

### Science brief

- Science brief: fission is the splitting of a heavy nucleus into lighter nuclei, releasing energy and typically neutrons.
- Science brief: fission of a heavy nucleus releases energy because the products are more tightly bound per nucleon than the original nucleus.
- Science brief: released neutrons can induce further fissions, which is the basis of a chain reaction.
- Science brief: control of a fission reactor works by controlling the number of neutrons available to induce further fission.
