## The science information and exact work for this lesson

### Science brief

- Science brief: a system at equilibrium responds to a change in conditions by shifting in the direction that partially counteracts that change.
- Science brief: changing concentration, pressure, or temperature disturbs the balance of forward and reverse rates, and the system shifts until they are equal again.
- Science brief: the shift partially offsets the change; it does not restore the original conditions.
- Science brief: temperature is the only one of these changes that alters the equilibrium position by changing the balance of the reaction's own energetics rather than by changing amounts.
