## The science information and exact work for this lesson

### Science brief

- Science brief: for a reaction to occur, particles must collide with sufficient energy and in a suitable orientation.
- Science brief: reaction rate depends on how often effective collisions occur, where effective means both energetic enough and correctly oriented.
- Science brief: activation energy is the minimum energy a collision must have to lead to reaction.
- Science brief: anything that raises the frequency or the effectiveness of collisions raises the rate.

### Evidence check: collision model of reactions
