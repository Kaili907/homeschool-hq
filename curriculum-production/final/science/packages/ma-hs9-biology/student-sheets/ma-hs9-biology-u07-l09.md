### Science brief

- Science brief: in mutualism both species benefit; in commensalism one benefits and the other is essentially unaffected; in parasitism and predation one benefits at the other's expense; in competition both are harmed.
- Science brief: interactions are classified by the effect on each participant, not by intent or by how the relationship appears.
- Science brief: interspecific competition occurs where species use the same limiting resource, and it can shift the resources each species uses.
- Science brief: predator and prey populations influence one another, typically with a delay between the change in one and the response of the other.
