### Science brief

- Science brief: homeostasis maintains internal conditions within a tolerance range, not at a single unchanging value.
- Science brief: a regulated variable has a setpoint and a range within which the organism functions.
- Science brief: values fluctuate within the range continuously; regulation acts when a departure is detected, so variation is expected.
- Science brief: different variables have different ranges, and exceeding a range has consequences specific to that variable.
