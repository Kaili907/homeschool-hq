### Science brief

- Science brief: all living organisms are made of cells, the cell is the basic unit of life, and all cells come from pre-existing cells.
- Science brief: prokaryotic cells have no nucleus or membrane-bound organelles; eukaryotic cells have both.
- Science brief: cell theory holds that organisms are composed of cells, that the cell is the basic unit of structure and function, and that cells arise only from existing cells.
- Science brief: prokaryotic cells lack a nucleus and membrane-bound organelles; eukaryotic cells have a nucleus and a range of organelles.
