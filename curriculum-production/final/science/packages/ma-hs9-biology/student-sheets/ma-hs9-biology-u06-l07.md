## The science information and exact work for this lesson

Everything required below is printed on this sheet. You do not need an unseen case, model, table, prior answer, or outside source.

### Science brief

- Science brief: only a small fraction of the energy at one trophic level, on the order of ten per cent, is incorporated into the next.
- Science brief: energy enters most ecosystems through producers and passes upward through consumers, with most released as thermal energy at each transfer.
- Science brief: because so little energy passes upward, food chains have few levels and higher levels support far less biomass.
- Science brief: energy flows through an ecosystem in one direction and is not recycled.
