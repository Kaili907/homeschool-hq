### Science brief

- Science brief: selection is detected as a change in the distribution of a trait in a population over generations.
- Science brief: a shift in a distribution is evidence about the population, not about any individual within it.
- Science brief: the strength of the evidence depends on sample size, on measurement method, and on the number of generations observed.
