**Accepted relationships and models.** What this lesson's learning target asserts:

- Individuals in a past population varied, just as individuals do today.
- A variation that helped individuals survive and reproduce in their environment could become more common in later generations.
- Which variation counts as an advantage is set by the environment, not by the organism.

**Disqualifying errors.** Each of these is `Not yet` on Scientific correctness however well the reasoning is documented and however complete the evidence is:

- Claiming an individual organism changes its own traits to suit its environment.
- Claiming a trait appeared because the organism needed it.
- Claiming an advantage in one environment is an advantage everywhere.

**Grade boundary.** The mechanism of natural selection and the genetics behind it are Grade 8 and High School work.
