### Science brief

- Science brief: offspring traits can be compared with parent traits to see which are similar and which differ.
- Science brief: a trait can appear in an offspring that neither parent shows.
- Science brief: resemblance to a parent is evidence of inheritance; it does not establish which parent a particular trait came from.
