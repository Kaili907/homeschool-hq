## The science information and exact work for this lesson

Everything required below is printed on this sheet. You do not need an unseen case, model, table, prior answer, or outside source.

### Science brief

- Science brief: wave speed equals frequency multiplied by wavelength.
- Science brief: in a given medium the wave speed is fixed by the medium, so frequency and wavelength vary inversely with each other.
- Science brief: when a wave crosses into a new medium its frequency is unchanged, set by the source, while its speed and wavelength both change.
- Science brief: the relationship holds for all waves, mechanical and electromagnetic alike.
