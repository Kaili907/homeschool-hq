## The science information and exact work for this lesson

Everything required below is printed on this sheet. You do not need an unseen case, model, table, prior answer, or outside source.

### Science brief

- Science brief: kinetic energy depends on mass and on the square of the speed; gravitational potential energy near Earth's surface depends on mass, gravitational field strength, and height above a chosen reference.
- Science brief: doubling an object's speed quadruples its kinetic energy, which is why stopping distance grows so steeply with speed.
- Science brief: potential energy is defined relative to a chosen reference level, and only differences in it are physically meaningful.
- Science brief: potential energy belongs to a system of interacting objects, such as an object and Earth, not to one object alone.
