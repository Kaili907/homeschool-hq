## The science information and exact work for this lesson

Everything required below is printed on this sheet. You do not need an unseen case, model, table, prior answer, or outside source.

### Science brief

- Science brief: when a wave meets a boundary it is reflected, absorbed, transmitted, or some combination of the three.
- Science brief: at a boundary, wave energy is divided between reflection, absorption, and transmission, and the division depends on both materials and on the wave.
- Science brief: absorbed wave energy is transferred to the material, usually raising its temperature.
- Science brief: the same material can reflect one kind of wave and transmit another, which is why glass is transparent to visible light but not to all radiation.
