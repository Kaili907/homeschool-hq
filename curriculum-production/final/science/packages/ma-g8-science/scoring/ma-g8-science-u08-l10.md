**Fixed for this topic.** These are settled, and work that contradicts one is `Not yet` on Scientific correctness:

- In artificial selection humans choose which individuals reproduce; in natural selection the environment determines differential reproductive success.

**Accepted relationships and models.** What this lesson's learning target asserts:

- Artificial selection works on the same heritable variation that natural selection acts on; only the selecting agent differs.
- Selecting strongly for one trait often carries other traits with it and can reduce genetic variety in the population.
- The speed of change under artificial selection shows how quickly heritable traits in a population can shift.

**Also correct.** Accept any of these framings — do not require the wording above:

- Selective breeding and artificial selection are the same idea.

**Disqualifying errors.** Each of these is `Not yet` on Scientific correctness however well the reasoning is documented and however complete the evidence is:

- Claiming artificial selection creates the desired variation rather than selecting from existing variation.
- Claiming artificial selection has no unintended effects on other traits or on genetic variety.
- Confusing artificial selection with direct genetic modification.

**Grade boundary.** Quantitative genetics, breeding values, and genetic engineering techniques are High School work.
