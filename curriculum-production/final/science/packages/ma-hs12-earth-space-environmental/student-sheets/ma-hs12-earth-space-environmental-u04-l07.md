## The science information and exact work for this lesson

Everything required below is printed on this sheet. You do not need an unseen case, model, table, prior answer, or outside source.

### Science brief

- Science brief: gravity provides the force that keeps a body in orbit, and an orbiting body is continuously accelerating toward the body it orbits.
- Science brief: an orbit is the result of a body's sideways motion combined with continuous gravitational acceleration toward the central body.
- Science brief: an orbiting object has weight; it appears weightless because it and everything with it are in free fall together.
- Science brief: the two bodies attract each other equally and both orbit their common centre of mass, which lies inside the more massive body when the mass difference is large.
