## The science information and exact work for this lesson

Everything required below is printed on this sheet. You do not need an unseen case, model, table, prior answer, or outside source.

### Science brief

- Science brief: earth's mantle is solid rock that deforms and flows very slowly; it is not a liquid layer.
- Science brief: heat from Earth's interior, from residual formation heat and radioactive decay, drives slow convective flow in the mantle.
- Science brief: mantle flow, together with the pull of dense subducting slabs and the push at ridges, drives plate motion.
- Science brief: the mantle behaves as a solid on short timescales and flows on geological ones, which is why seismic shear waves pass through it.
