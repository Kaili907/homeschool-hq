**Fixed for this topic.** These are settled, and work that contradicts one is `Not yet` on Scientific correctness:

- Weathering breaks rock down in place; erosion transports the products; deposition lays them down elsewhere.

**Accepted relationships and models.** What this lesson's learning target asserts:

- Physical weathering fragments rock without changing its composition; chemical weathering alters the minerals themselves.
- Rates depend on climate, rock type, slope, and vegetation cover, so they vary greatly between settings.
- Chemical weathering of silicate rock consumes atmospheric carbon dioxide, linking surface processes to the long-term carbon cycle.

**Also correct.** Accept any of these framings — do not require the wording above:

- Physical and mechanical weathering are the same category.

**Disqualifying errors.** Each of these is `Not yet` on Scientific correctness however well the reasoning is documented and however complete the evidence is:

- Using weathering and erosion interchangeably.
- Claiming chemical weathering rates are independent of climate.
- Claiming weathering rates are uniform across all rock types and settings.
