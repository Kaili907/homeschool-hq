# Standards Coverage — Arts and Music, Grades 9–12

**Framework:** Michigan Arts Education Standards (National Core Arts Standards)

NCAS separates **artistic processes** from **artistic disciplines**, and the
process names differ by discipline: Music and Theatre use *Performing*, Visual
Arts uses *Presenting*, and Media Arts uses *Producing*. The two axes are
reported separately below rather than mixed into one list. Anchors are recorded
as prose labels, matching the Grade 5/7/8 release convention — no state standard
codes are invented.

## Disciplines out of scope

**Dance is out of scope for this sequence.** NCAS and the Michigan standards
both include Dance as a discipline, but these four courses teach no
choreography, movement vocabulary, or dance technique, so no unit claims Dance
coverage. A family needing Dance should treat it as a separate course. An
earlier draft anchored one unit to "Dance — Performing" on the strength of a
single "physical expression and staging" topic; that anchor was false and has
been removed.
