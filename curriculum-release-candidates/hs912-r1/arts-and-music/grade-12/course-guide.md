# Grade 12 Arts and Music — Course Guide

**Course ID:** `ma-g12-arts-and-music`  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Instructional sessions:** 72  
**Typical session:** 45–75 minutes (60–90 in capstone units)  
**Cadence:** twice weekly

## Course description

A Grade 12 arts and music course completing the 9-12 progression through a proposed, produced, refined, presented, and defended capstone portfolio, with professional practice and pathway planning placed early enough to meet real application and audition deadlines.

## Course outcomes

By the end of the course, learners will:

1. Demonstrate the listed Michigan-aligned knowledge and practices through independent, accessible evidence.
2. Explain reasoning, cite evidence, document process, or justify design choices rather than supply unsupported answers.
3. Transfer learning to an unfamiliar task, text, situation, dataset, performance, or project.
4. Use feedback and error evidence to revise without shame or character judgments.
5. Complete the course capstone: **A substantial Grade 12 arts capstone portfolio with a sustained body of original work, an artist statement, a curated presentation in a private-capable format, complete rights and licensing documentation, professional practice materials, and a defense.**

## Instructional model

Six-session units run: Launch and diagnostic; Explicit model; Guided practice; Application or project; Mastery check; Correction and reflection. Twelve-session units run: Launch and diagnostic; Concept model A; Guided practice A; Independent application A; Concept model B; Guided practice B; Investigation or close reading; Reteach and varied practice; Performance task build; Synthesis and review; Unit assessment; Correction and reflection.

Every topic in a unit receives at least one instructional lesson before any
assessment or correction lesson. Mastery-check, assessment, synthesis, and
correction lessons are scoped to the whole unit rather than to a single topic.
Lessons are resumable by segment, media is optional, and a readable fallback is
always specified.

## Mastery and grading

- One correct answer never establishes mastery.
- Unit assessments combine concept, representation or source use, application, error or claim analysis, connection, performance evidence, and reflection. Capstone units add a defense-and-response-to-critique prompt.
- Suggested reporting: **Secure**, **Developing**, or **Not Yet**, supported by evidence rather than a single percentage.
- Reassessment uses fresh items or a new application after targeted instruction.
- Approved breaks, accommodations, voice/no-voice choices, and alternate response modes are not failures.
- Parent summaries show target, completion, evidence type, and next step; they exclude raw private reflections, diagnosis language, and unnecessary personal data.

## Scope and sequence

| Unit | Title | Days | Standards anchors | Performance task |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Capstone Concept, Research, and Proposal | 12 | Michigan Arts Education Standards: Visual Arts — Creating; Music — Creating | Write and defend a capstone proposal, in writing or in a chosen private or shared format, with artistic intent, precedent research, scope, and a rights plan. |
| 2 | Professional Practice, Rights, and Pathways | 12 | Michigan Arts Education Standards: Visual Arts — Connecting; Media Arts — Connecting | Assemble professional practice materials with a rights and licensing plan and a pathway action plan, timed to real application and audition deadlines. |
| 3 | Sustained Studio or Composition Production | 12 | Michigan Arts Education Standards: Visual Arts — Creating; Media Arts — Producing | Reach a capstone production milestone with documented process, a midpoint critique, and a revision plan. |
| 4 | Advanced Craft, Revision, and Mastery | 12 | Michigan Arts Education Standards: Visual Arts — Creating; Music — Performing | Complete a refined capstone body of work with documented revision cycles against explicit quality standards. |
| 5 | Exhibition, Recital, or Private Presentation Design | 12 | Michigan Arts Education Standards: Visual Arts — Presenting; Theatre — Performing | Design and execute a capstone presentation in a chosen private or public format with full access supports. |
| 6 | Capstone Defense, Portfolio Completion, and Transition | 12 | Michigan Arts Education Standards: Visual Arts — Responding; Visual Arts — Presenting | Defend the completed capstone portfolio in a chosen private or public mode, with a final statement, a critical response, and a transition plan. |

## Capstone

A substantial Grade 12 arts capstone portfolio with a sustained body of original work, an artist statement, a curated presentation in a private-capable format, complete rights and licensing documentation, professional practice materials, and a defense.

The capstone may be presented publicly, privately, in writing, orally, through a demonstration, or in another accessible format that preserves the learning targets.
