# Michigan High School Social Studies — Standards Reference

**Source document:** Michigan K-12 Social Studies Standards, v 6/19, Michigan Department of Education
**Credit requirement source:** Michigan Merit Curriculum social studies course/credit requirement, Michigan Department of Education

This file records the high school organization of the Michigan standards as published, so that every
code used in this package can be traced. Structure and code numbering are transcribed from the source
document. No expectation text is reproduced; consult the source document for the wording of each
expectation.

## Social Studies Process and Skills (repeated in each course/credit standard)

- **P1** Reading and Communication
- **P2** Inquiry, Research, and Analysis
- **P3** Public Discourse and Decision Making
- **P4** Civic Participation

## United States History and Geography (USHG)

High school USHG covers Foundations and Eras 6 through 9. Era 6.1 is introduced in Grade 8 and begins
the high school sequence.

- **F1** Political and Intellectual Transformations of America to 1877 — F1.1, F1.2, F1.3
- **USHG Era 6 — The Development of an Industrial, Urban, and Global United States (1870–1930)**
  - 6.1 Growth of an Industrial and Urban America — 6.1.1–6.1.4
  - 6.2 Becoming a World Power — 6.2.1–6.2.4
  - 6.3 Progressive Era — 6.3.1–6.3.4
- **USHG Era 7 — The Great Depression and World War II (1920–1945)**
  - 7.1 Growing Crisis of Industrial Capitalism and Responses — 7.1.1–7.1.3
  - 7.2 World War II — 7.2.1–7.2.4
- **USHG Era 8 — Post-World War II United States (1945–1989)**
  - 8.1 Cold War and the United States — 8.1.1–8.1.3
  - 8.2 Domestic Changes and Policies — 8.2.1–8.2.4
  - 8.3 Civil Rights in the Post-World War II Era — 8.3.1–8.3.5
- **USHG Era 9 — America in a New Global Age**
  - 9.1 Impact of Globalization on the United States — 9.1.1–9.1.2
  - 9.2 Changes in America's Role in the World — 9.2.1–9.2.2
  - 9.3 Policy Debates — 9.3.1

## World History and Geography (WHG)

High school WHG covers Foundations, Eras 4 through 7, and Contemporary Global Issues. Some Era 4
content is taught in Grade 7.

- **F1** World Historical and Geographical Inquiry and Literacy Practices
- **WHG Era 4 — Expanding and Intensified Hemispheric Interactions, 300–1500 CE**
  - 4.1 Growth and Interactions of World Religions, and Intensifying Trade Networks and Contacts — 4.1.1–4.1.2
  - 4.2 Growth of Islam and Dar al-Islam, Unification of Eurasia under the Mongols, and Spheres of Interaction and Influence in the Americas — 4.2.1–4.2.3
- **WHG Era 5 — The Emergence of the First Global Age, 15th to 18th Centuries**
  - 5.1 Emerging Global System and Diffusion of World Religions — 5.1.1–5.1.2
  - 5.2 Cultural Encounters and the Columbian Exchange, the Trans-Atlantic Slave Trade, and Afro-Eurasian Empires — 5.2.1–5.2.3
- **WHG Era 6 — An Age of Global Revolutions, 18th Century–1914**
  - 6.1 Global Revolutions, Worldwide Migrations and Population Changes, and Increasing Global Interconnections — 6.1.1–6.1.3
  - 6.2 Comparing Political Revolutions and/or Independence Movements, Growth of Nationalism and Nation-States, Industrialization, and Imperialism — 6.2.1–6.2.4
- **WHG Era 7 — Global Crisis and Achievement, 1900–Present Day**
  - 7.1 Power and Resistance, Global Conflict, Genocide in the 20th Century, and Technological, Scientific, and Cultural Exchanges — 7.1.1–7.1.4
  - 7.2 World War I, Interwar Period, World War II, Cold War Conflicts, Revolution, Decolonization, and Democratization, and Case Studies of Genocide — 7.2.1–7.2.6
- **Contemporary Global Issues** — CG1 Population, CG2 Resources, CG3 Patterns of Global Interactions, CG4 Conflict, Cooperation, and Security

## Michigan Merit Curriculum social studies requirement

Three social studies credits, comprising at least:

| Component | Credit |
| --- | --- |
| United States History and Geography | 1.0 |
| World History and Geography | 1.0 |
| Economics | 0.5 |
| Civics | 0.5 |

The Michigan Department of Education states that no specific courses are required to meet the social
studies requirement, and that credit may be earned through traditional courses or through courses that
integrate the social studies standards. **Michigan does not assign these credits to particular grade
levels.** Grade placement in this package is a Manuel Academy decision.
