### Session 1 — Launch and diagnostic: historical and geographic inquiry
**Lesson ID:** `ma-g9-social-studies-u01-l01`

**Focus:** historical and geographic inquiry  
**Objective:** Activate prior knowledge about historical and geographic inquiry, surface initial thinking without penalty, and establish the unit question.

**Student activity:** Learner completes a new application of historical and geographic inquiry and records the result, the reasoning or evidence that produced it, and a citation for every source used.

**Exit ticket:** In one concise response, show or explain the most important idea about historical and geographic inquiry; then identify one check that would catch an error, an unsupported claim, or an uncorroborated source.

**Mastery rule:** Do not mark mastery from one answer. Use source analysis, map or data work, discussion, an evidence-based claim with citation, and an inquiry product; require accurate independent evidence and successful transfer or retrieval on at least two separate occasions.
