## Unit 1: Research Design and the Arc of Inquiry

**Standards:** P1, P2, Civics C6.4.1, Civics C6.4.2  
**Essential question:** What makes a research question worth asking and actually answerable?  
**Performance task:** Produce a reviewed research proposal with a compelling question, supporting questions, candidate sources, methods, and ethical safeguards.
