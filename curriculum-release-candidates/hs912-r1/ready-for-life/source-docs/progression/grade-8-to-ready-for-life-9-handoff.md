# Grade 8 → Ready for Life 9 — Handoff

## What Grade 8 leaves

The published Grade 8 Ready for Life course
(`curriculum-content/manuel-academy/1.0.0/grades/grade-8/courses/ready-for-life`) is **not
modified, moved, renumbered, re-tagged, or reproduced by this lane.** It is read for
continuity only.

Its six units are:

| Unit | Title |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Independent Planning and Executive Routines |
| 2 | Home Operations and Basic Maintenance |
| 3 | Meal Planning, Cooking, and Food Management |
| 4 | Transportation, Appointments, and Community Navigation |
| 5 | Work, Communication, and Career Readiness |
| 6 | Independent-Living Capstone |

Shape: 6 units × 6 lessons = 36 lessons. Across the three published grades (5, 7, 8) that is
the 18-unit, 108-lesson Ready for Life body the Family Pilot lane integrates.

Its standards field is the literal string `Manuel Academy RFL Grade 8 progression` — an
honest local declaration, because Ready for Life has no external standards body.

## What Ready for Life 9 picks up

1. **The same shape.** 6 units × 6 lessons = 36 lessons. Pacing and unit boundaries carry over
   unchanged. The validator asserts this against the Grade 8 file directly.
2. **The same local-declaration convention.** Grade 9 carries
   `Manuel Academy RFL Grade 9 progression`. No external standard is claimed at any grade,
   because none exists. See `../standards/manuel-academy-rfl-9-12-competencies.json`.
3. **The same safety posture,** extended rather than relaxed: no shame, guardian supervision
   for hazardous tasks, no forced disclosure, no photo or recording requirement.
4. **The capstone habit.** Grade 8 ends in an Independent-Living Capstone. Every high-school
   year likewise ends in a capstone, and Grade 12's is the senior transition capstone.

## What changes at Grade 9

Grade 8 establishes routines under adult direction. Grade 9 converts them into **systems the
learner owns and keeps running** — a planning system that survives a hard week, two household
systems taken over with documented handover, a week of meals planned and cooked, a round trip
planned backwards from an arrival time with a contingency.

The Grade 8 unit topics map forward as follows, so nothing is dropped:

| Grade 8 unit | Continues into |
| --- | --- |
| Independent Planning and Executive Routines | G9 U1 Planning Systems; G11 U5 Problem Solving; G12 U1 Adult-Life Operating System |
| Home Operations and Basic Maintenance | G9 U2 Household Independence; G11 U4 Housing; G12 U2 Independent Living Simulation |
| Meal Planning, Cooking, and Food Management | G9 U3 Food Systems; G12 U2 Independent Living Simulation |
| Transportation, Appointments, and Community Navigation | G9 U4 Transportation; G10 U5 Civic Participation; G12 U4 Civic and Legal Adulthood |
| Work, Communication, and Career Readiness | G9 U5 Communication; G10 U1-U3 Career, Job Readiness, Professional Communication; G12 U3 Workplace Entry |
| Independent-Living Capstone | every year capstone, culminating in the G12 Transition-to-Adulthood Capstone |

## What does not change, ever

No shame. No unsafe unsupervised real-world task. Guardian sign-off where a real-world task is
claimed as evidence, and a click never certifies one. No credential collection. No forced
private disclosure. These hold in Grade 8 and in all four high-school years, and the validator
enforces each of them on every one of the 144 high-school lessons.
