# Manuel Academy — Ready for Life, Grade 9

**Systems for Independence.** Grade 9 turns the Grade 8 routines into durable personal systems the learner runs themselves, with supervision where a task carries real risk.

- Course id: `ma-g9-ready-for-life`
- Units: 6  |  Lessons: 36  |  Schedule: 36 weeks x 1 day
- Competency framework: local Manuel Academy progression (`Manuel Academy RFL Grade 9 progression`). Ready for Life has no external standards body and none is claimed.

## Scope and sequence

### Unit 1 — Planning Systems and Time Management

*What planning system does this learner actually keep using when a week gets hard?*

- Days: 6  |  Domains: RFL.PLAN, RFL.SOLVE
- Topics: choosing a calendar and task system that fits; capturing commitments before they are forgotten; breaking a multi-week obligation into steps; estimating how long a task really takes; protecting sleep, study, and recovery time; reviewing a week and adjusting the system
- Performance task: Run a personal planning system for two weeks, then report what it caught, what it missed, and one change made.

### Unit 2 — Household Independence and Home Systems

*Which household systems can this learner keep running without being reminded?*

- Days: 6  |  Domains: RFL.HOME
- Topics: laundry, cleaning, and supply rotation as a system; safe use of household appliances with supervision; recognising a repair that needs a qualified adult; routine upkeep and seasonal tasks; storing and labelling supplies safely; handing a task back with a clear status
- Performance task: Take documented ownership of two recurring household systems for two weeks with adult review.

### Unit 3 — Food Systems: Planning, Shopping, and Cooking

*How does a person get a week of decent meals planned, bought, and cooked safely?*

- Days: 6  |  Domains: RFL.FOOD
- Topics: planning meals across a week; building a shopping list from a plan; food safety, storage, and expiry; knife and heat safety under supervision; cooking three balanced meals; reducing waste and using leftovers
- Performance task: Plan, shop for, and prepare three balanced meals across a week with adult supervision for heat and knives.

### Unit 4 — Transportation and Trip Planning

*How does a person get somewhere unfamiliar, on time, with a backup plan?*

- Days: 6  |  Domains: RFL.MOVE
- Topics: reading schedules, maps, and routes; planning a trip backwards from an arrival time; comparing travel options on time, cost, and reliability; what to do when a connection fails; personal safety and check-in agreements; local rules for travel and identification
- Performance task: Plan a complete round trip to an unfamiliar destination including timing, cost, contingency, and a check-in plan, and complete it with the agreed supervision.

### Unit 5 — Communication and Self-Advocacy

*How does this learner ask for what they need, clearly and without apology?*

- Days: 6  |  Domains: RFL.COMM
- Topics: stating a need clearly and specifically; asking a question of an unfamiliar adult; making and confirming an appointment; writing a short, clear message that gets a response; listening, confirming, and taking notes; following up when there is no reply
- Performance task: Prepare and carry out one real request or appointment conversation with adult support, then evaluate how it went.

### Unit 6 — Grade 9 Independence Capstone

*Can this learner run their own week — plan, home, food, travel, and communication — and show it?*

- Days: 6  |  Domains: RFL.PLAN, RFL.ADULT
- Topics: integrating the year's systems into one week; documenting evidence without oversharing; adult attestation of real-world tasks; identifying what still needs support; planning the next step honestly; presenting the week and defending the choices
- Performance task: Run a documented one-week independence plan covering planning, a household system, meals, one trip, and one communication task, with adult attestation and a written review.
- This is the Grade 9 year capstone.

## Safety and dignity policies

**No shame.** Difficulty with a life skill is never treated as a character flaw. Honest self-assessment scores full marks. No learner is compared to another, and no result is framed as a personal or family failing.

**No unsafe unsupervised task.** Any task involving heat, sharp tools, appliances, chemicals, medication, electricity, heights, power tools, driving, water, money movement, or unfamiliar adults is guardian-supervised or simulated. Where supervision is unavailable, the simulated alternative carries **equal credit**.

**Guardian sign-off, and what a click cannot do.** A click cannot certify a real-world adult-supervised task. A learner-side completion toggle records only that the learner reports having finished. Evidence for this lesson is not complete until an adult attestation names the observing adult's role, what the adult actually observed, and the date.

Lessons requiring attestation in this course: day 4 and day 6 of every unit (12 of 36 lessons).

**No credential collection.** No password, account number, government identifier, card number, or real balance is requested, displayed, entered, or stored anywhere in this course.

**No forced private disclosure.** Autobiographical, household, financial, or health disclosure is never required. A fictional or analytical alternative is always available and never scored lower.

**Not advice.** Nothing here is medical, legal, financial, employment, or immigration advice. Local rules vary and are to be verified against a current local source rather than assumed.

**Mastery.** Practice generation never directly awards mastery. Do not mark mastery from one answer, and never from practice generation alone. Require accurate independent evidence plus the learner's own explanation on at least two separate occasions, at least one of them transferred to a situation the learner has not rehearsed. A guardian-attested real-world performance counts as one occasion only when the attestation is present.

**Guardian visibility.** Share the lesson target, completion state, evidence type, guardian attestation where required, and the next instructional step. Do not expose raw reflections, raw responses, recordings, photographs, household detail, health detail, or diagnosis-like language.
