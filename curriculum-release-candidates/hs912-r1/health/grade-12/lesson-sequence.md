# Grade 12 Health — Lesson Sequence

36 instructional days.

| Day | Unit | Day in unit | Phase | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 | 1 | Launch and diagnostic | building routines that survive a schedule change |
| 2 | 1 | 2 | Explicit model | sleep, movement, fueling, and recovery as maintained systems rather than goals |
| 3 | 1 | 3 | Guided practice | tracking what matters without measuring the body |
| 4 | 1 | 4 | Application or project | managing a routine through illness, stress, or a heavy period |
| 5 | 1 | 5 | Mastery check | restarting after a routine collapses, without self-punishment |
| 6 | 1 | 6 | Correction and reflection | deciding what to keep, drop, and add each season |
| 7 | 2 | 1 | Launch and diagnostic | the shape of adult relationships: chosen, professional, and civic |
| 8 | 2 | 2 | Explicit model | holding boundaries with roommates, coworkers, and supervisors |
| 9 | 2 | 3 | Guided practice | consent and respect in adult contexts |
| 10 | 2 | 4 | Application or project | recognizing coercive control and financial or social pressure |
| 11 | 2 | 5 | Mastery check | leaving an unsafe situation with a plan |
| 12 | 2 | 6 | Correction and reflection | building a support network deliberately rather than by accident |
| 13 | 3 | 1 | Launch and diagnostic | transition stress and why capable people struggle during it |
| 14 | 3 | 2 | Explicit model | maintenance strategies versus emergency strategies |
| 15 | 3 | 3 | Guided practice | sleep, isolation, and workload as leading indicators |
| 16 | 3 | 4 | Application or project | when to seek professional support and how to start that process as an adult |
| 17 | 3 | 5 | Mastery check | cost, access, and confidentiality of adult mental-health care |
| 18 | 3 | 6 | Correction and reflection | supporting others while protecting your own capacity |
| 19 | 4 | 1 | Launch and diagnostic | maintaining CPR/AED and first-aid readiness and where to get certified |
| 20 | 4 | 2 | Explicit model | home, vehicle, workplace, and travel safety systems |
| 21 | 4 | 3 | Guided practice | risk assessment for an unfamiliar situation |
| 22 | 4 | 4 | Application or project | impaired driving, fatigue, and transportation decisions |
| 23 | 4 | 5 | Mastery check | emergency communication, documents, and a household plan |
| 24 | 4 | 6 | Correction and reflection | knowing the limits of what a bystander should attempt |
| 25 | 5 | 1 | Launch and diagnostic | choosing and establishing with a primary care provider |
| 26 | 5 | 2 | Explicit model | coverage options after high school and what each actually covers |
| 27 | 5 | 3 | Guided practice | reading a bill, an explanation of benefits, and a denial |
| 28 | 5 | 4 | Application or project | appealing a denial and asking for an itemized correction |
| 29 | 5 | 5 | Mastery check | prescription cost, generics, and assistance programs |
| 30 | 5 | 6 | Correction and reflection | recognizing predatory health marketing aimed at young adults |
| 31 | 6 | 1 | Launch and diagnostic | integrating four years of health learning into one working plan |
| 32 | 6 | 2 | Explicit model | stating evidence for each element of the plan |
| 33 | 6 | 3 | Guided practice | building in review points and revision triggers |
| 34 | 6 | 4 | Application or project | choosing one advocacy commitment that is realistic |
| 35 | 6 | 5 | Mastery check | presenting a plan to a guardian without disclosing private detail |
| 36 | 6 | 6 | Correction and reflection | defending choices under questioning and revising after critique |
