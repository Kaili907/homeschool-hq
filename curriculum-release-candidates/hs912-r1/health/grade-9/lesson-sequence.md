# Grade 9 Health — Lesson Sequence

36 instructional days.

| Day | Unit | Day in unit | Phase | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 | 1 | Launch and diagnostic | what health literacy means at the high-school level |
| 2 | 1 | 2 | Explicit model | internal and external influences on a health decision |
| 3 | 1 | 3 | Guided practice | source quality: authorship, evidence, currency, and conflict of interest |
| 4 | 1 | 4 | Application or project | a repeatable decision process for health choices |
| 5 | 1 | 5 | Mastery check | short-term convenience versus longer-term consequence |
| 6 | 1 | 6 | Correction and reflection | reviewing a decision after the fact without self-blame |
| 7 | 2 | 1 | Launch and diagnostic | the stress response and what it does to attention, sleep, and mood |
| 8 | 2 | 2 | Explicit model | coping strategies that are sustainable versus strategies that cost more later |
| 9 | 2 | 3 | Guided practice | building a personal regulation routine |
| 10 | 2 | 4 | Application or project | recognizing when a difficulty is bigger than self-management |
| 11 | 2 | 5 | Mastery check | trusted adults, school supports, and crisis lines |
| 12 | 2 | 6 | Correction and reflection | supporting a peer without taking responsibility for their care |
| 13 | 3 | 1 | Launch and diagnostic | recognizing an emergency and activating help |
| 14 | 3 | 2 | Explicit model | CPR and AED awareness: what they are, when they are used, and how to get trained |
| 15 | 3 | 3 | Guided practice | bleeding control, choking response, and basic first aid concepts |
| 16 | 3 | 4 | Application or project | concussion and head-injury recognition and the stop rule |
| 17 | 3 | 5 | Mastery check | vehicle, water, fire, and home-hazard prevention |
| 18 | 3 | 6 | Correction and reflection | bystander responsibility and safe intervention limits |
| 19 | 4 | 1 | Launch and diagnostic | physiology of alcohol, nicotine and vaping products, and cannabis on the adolescent body and brain |
| 20 | 4 | 2 | Explicit model | dependence, tolerance, and why adolescent timing matters |
| 21 | 4 | 3 | Guided practice | marketing, social media, and normalization pressure |
| 22 | 4 | 4 | Application or project | prescription and over-the-counter medication safety and storage |
| 23 | 4 | 5 | Mastery check | refusal and exit strategies that survive real social pressure |
| 24 | 4 | 6 | Correction and reflection | overdose and poisoning response, and where families get help |
| 25 | 5 | 1 | Launch and diagnostic | adequate fueling for growth, school load, and activity |
| 26 | 5 | 2 | Explicit model | hydration across a day |
| 27 | 5 | 3 | Guided practice | sleep need, sleep debt, and circadian timing in high school |
| 28 | 5 | 4 | Application or project | recovery after effort, illness, or a heavy week |
| 29 | 5 | 5 | Mastery check | evaluating supplement, energy-product, and fitness-industry claims |
| 30 | 5 | 6 | Correction and reflection | body respect and resisting appearance-based health messaging |
| 31 | 6 | 1 | Launch and diagnostic | daily wellness systems: hygiene, dental, vision, hearing, and skin care |
| 32 | 6 | 2 | Explicit model | how communicable conditions transmit and how transmission is interrupted |
| 33 | 6 | 3 | Guided practice | HIV: accurate transmission and non-transmission facts, testing, and treatment as prevention |
| 34 | 6 | 4 | Application or project | immunization concepts and where records live |
| 35 | 6 | 5 | Mastery check | stigma, accuracy, and respectful language about health conditions |
| 36 | 6 | 6 | Correction and reflection | routine care and screening as ordinary adult maintenance |
