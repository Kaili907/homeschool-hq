## The distinction this document exists to make

**Michigan's minimum and Manuel Academy's provision are different things, and this package deliberately exceeds the minimum.**

### What Michigan requires

- **Michigan Merit Curriculum (public school districts and public school academies):** at least **one credit** in subject matter that includes **both health and physical education**. It may be met as two half-credit courses — ½ health and ½ PE. An alternative route allows ½ credit in health plus ½ credit awarded for approved participation in extracurricular athletics or other extracurricular physical activity. How the credit is offered is a local decision, and it may be earned at any point in a student's high-school career.
- **MCL 380.1502** requires health and physical education to be established and provided in all public schools.
- **Michigan homeschools and nonpublic schools are not bound by the Michigan Merit Curriculum at all.** Michigan's home-education requirement is instruction in mathematics, history, reading, civics, writing, literature, English grammar, spelling, and science — health and physical education are **not** on that list. For students aged 14 and over there is an additional requirement covering the US and Michigan Constitutions and civil government.

So for a Michigan homeschool, the *legally required* amount of high-school health and physical education is **zero**, and for a Michigan public-school student it is **one combined credit, earned once**.

### What Manuel Academy provides

**Health and physical education every year, in all four high-school years, as a deliberate curricular choice — not because a minimum requires it.**

Four years of health (144 days) and four years of PE (432 days) is roughly **three times** the Michigan Merit Curriculum's combined one-credit minimum, and it is provision where the homeschool minimum requires none. The reason is pedagogical rather than regulatory: the four-year arc is built so that Grade 12 hands the learner a health and movement system they run without adult scaffolding, and that outcome depends on repeated practice across years, which a single one-semester course cannot produce.

**Nothing in this package awards credit, and nothing in it demonstrates accreditation, state approval, or that any external institution will accept these courses.** Credit is issued by whoever issues the transcript.

## Credit recommendation

Using the common convention that **120 scheduled hours ≈ 1.0 credit** and **60 hours ≈ 0.5 credit**:

| | Per year | Four-year total | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Health (recommended)** | **0.25 credit** | **1.0 credit** | ~27 scheduled hours per year, plus untimed performance-task and capstone work |
| PE (recommended, see companion doc) | 0.5 credit | 2.0 credits | ~85.5 scheduled hours per year |
| **Combined** | **0.75 credit** | **3.0 credits** | against a Michigan Merit minimum of 1.0 credit total |

Two honest caveats on the health figure:

1. At ~108 scheduled hours across four years, health sits slightly **under** a strict 120-hour reading of 1.0 credit. The recommendation of 1.0 leans on the performance tasks and the Grade 12 capstone, which are untimed and substantial. A transcript issuer who counts scheduled hours only should record **0.75 credit** for four years of health instead, and that is a defensible reading of the same course set.
2. A family that only wants to mirror the Michigan Merit half-credit health element can bank **Grades 9 and 10 as 0.5 credit in health** and treat Grades 11 and 12 as an elective extension. The course sequence does not change.

## Mastery pacing

Every lesson's mastery rule requires evidence on **at least two occasions separated by time or representation**, and the validator enforces that no lesson or unit assessment claims mastery from a single occasion. A weekly cadence gives that separation naturally. A compressed block does not, so a family running health as a single term should plan a deliberate later retrieval pass — the Grade 12 capstone is designed to serve that role across the whole four years, but within a single compressed course the separation has to be scheduled on purpose.
