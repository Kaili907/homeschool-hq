## Unit 1: Mathematical Habits, Place Value, and Rounding

**Essential question:** How do we make sense of an unfamiliar problem, and how does place value help us round, add, and subtract within 1,000?  
**Standards:** 3.NBT.1, 3.NBT.2, MP.1, MP.3, MP.4, MP.6, MP.7  
**Fluency target:** Add and subtract within 1,000 using place-value strategies and algorithms.  
**Performance task:** Build a Number Sense Toolkit poster: for each strategy, show a worked example, a model, and a check for reasonableness.

### Day 1 (course day 1) - Launch and diagnostic: making sense of unfamiliar problems
**Lesson ID:** `ma-g3-mathematics-u01-l01`  
**Standards:** MP.1, MP.3  
**Evidence type:** instruction  
**Objective:** Build an accurate mental model of making sense of unfamiliar problems using a concrete or visual representation before any symbol work.

**Student activity:** The learner works alongside the adult or tutor to build and label a model of making sense of unfamiliar problems, then writes the matching notation and explains how the two match.

**Standards:** 3.NF.1, 3.MD.7, 3.MD.8, 3.G.1, 3.G.2, MP.1, MP.3, MP.4  
**Fluency target:** Solve perimeter problems for polygons, including finding an unknown side length, and classify quadrilaterals by attribute.  
**Performance task:** Capstone: design a small garden, room, or play space using perimeter, area, shape categories, and fractional partitioning, with a written justification of every decision.

### Day 1 (course day 163) - Launch and diagnostic: distance around compared with space inside
**Lesson ID:** `ma-g3-mathematics-u10-l01`  
**Standards:** 3.MD.8, MP.1  
**Evidence type:** instruction  
**Objective:** Build an accurate mental model of distance around compared with space inside using a concrete or visual representation before any symbol work.

**Student activity:** The learner works alongside the adult or tutor to build and label a model of distance around compared with space inside, then writes the matching notation and explains how the two match.

**Exit check:** Show or explain the main idea of distance around compared with space inside in one response, and name the part that still feels unsteady.

**Watch for:** Adds only the two labeled side lengths of a rectangle and reports that as the perimeter.
