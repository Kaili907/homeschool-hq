# Arts and Music — Standards Map (Grades 3–4)

**Jurisdictional focus:** Michigan
**Status:** Locally authored curriculum aligned to published standards; this package is not a claim of
state approval, accreditation, licensure, or automatic credit.

## Source

| Source | Official URL |
| --- | --- |
| Michigan Visual, Performing, and Applied Arts Standards | https://www.michigan.gov/mde/services/academic-standards/mmc/curriculum/arts |

Michigan's arts standards use four artistic-process anchor standards — Creating, Performing/Presenting/
Producing, Responding, and Connecting — applied across disciplines (Dance, Media Arts, Music, Theatre,
Visual Arts). Michigan's own standards document does not prescribe an exact grade-by-grade lesson split
within the K–5 band; the unit order and specific activities below are Manuel Academy's local curricular
sequencing decision, scaffolded so each artistic process reappears at increasing depth from Grade 3 to
Grade 4 to Grade 5.

## Mapping status

Every unit and lesson standards entry in this package uses human-authored, discipline-and-process labels
drawn directly from the source list above (no auto-generated or inferred codes). A human curriculum
reviewer should confirm final standard-to-lesson granularity against the published Michigan Visual,
Performing, and Applied Arts Standards document before this package is promoted out of
`curriculum-authoring/`.

## Vertical alignment

This Grade 3–4 sequence is the direct on-ramp to the existing Grade 5 Arts and Music course
(`curriculum-content/manuel-academy/1.0.0/grades/grade-5/courses/arts-and-music`), which uses the same four
artistic processes at greater depth (visual elements and observation, materials and techniques, music
foundations and listening, theater/storytelling/movement, arts/culture/context, and a portfolio/rehearsal/
exhibition capstone unit). Grade 3 introduces each process concretely with maximum choice and minimum
required performance; Grade 4 adds craftsmanship, listening analysis, and cultural context; Grade 5
formalizes portfolio curation and exhibition.
