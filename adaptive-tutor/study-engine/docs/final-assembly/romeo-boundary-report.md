# Romeo Virtual Academy adapter boundary

The Romeo adapter accepts only manually entered or separately approved-import assignment metadata. It can associate Manuel Academy tutoring support, a resume note, and a completion update proposal.

It does not accept or store usernames, passwords, tokens, cookies, login sessions, or secrets. It does not log in, scrape, synchronize automatically, claim production delivery, or become an instructional authority. Scheduling requires an explicit offset-bearing start, intended local date, household IANA timezone, and overlap disambiguation.

The public Session 9 entry point omits the historical DEC-018 reconciliation constant and raw generic resolver. Credential, unsupported-mode, invalid-timezone, impossible-date, and duplicate-update attempts reject or quarantine.
