Each 108-day course is designed to run **three lessons per week across a 36-week school year**, alongside the 36-day health course which runs one day per week. The two subjects together occupy four instructional slots per week.

Several units — the Grade 11 periodized cycle, the Grade 12 independent training cycle, the six-week Grade 10 program — run **across** calendar weeks rather than inside a single block of days. The 12-day unit structure is the instructional sequence, not a constraint on how long the learner's own training plan runs. A family should expect those units to overlap with the units that follow them.

## The distinction this document exists to make

**Michigan's minimum and Manuel Academy's provision are different things, and this package deliberately exceeds the minimum.**

### What Michigan requires

- **Michigan Merit Curriculum (public school districts and public school academies):** at least **one credit** in subject matter that includes **both health and physical education**, which may be met as two half-credit courses — ½ health and ½ PE. An alternative route allows ½ credit in health plus ½ credit awarded for **approved participation in extracurricular athletics or other extracurricular activities involving physical activity** — that is, Michigan already permits a student to satisfy the entire PE element without taking a PE course. The credit may be earned at any point in high school, and how it is offered is a local decision.
- **MCL 380.1502** requires health and physical education to be established and provided in all public schools.
- **Michigan homeschools and nonpublic schools are not bound by the Michigan Merit Curriculum at all.** Michigan's home-education subject requirement — mathematics, history, reading, civics, writing, literature, English grammar, spelling, and science — does **not** include physical education.

So for a Michigan homeschool, the *legally required* amount of high-school physical education is **zero**, and for a Michigan public-school student it is **half a credit, once, and it can be satisfied by extracurricular sport instead of instruction.**

### What Manuel Academy provides

**Physical education every year, in all four high-school years, as a deliberate curricular choice — not because a minimum requires it.**

432 instructional days of PE against a state minimum that can be discharged with a season of extracurricular sport is a deliberate departure, and the reason is what the four-year arc is for. Extracurricular participation produces activity; it does not reliably produce a learner who can design their own training cycle, adapt an activity for someone else's body, evaluate what a gym membership actually costs them, and keep moving through a year with no coach and no facility. Those are the Grade 11 and Grade 12 outcomes, and they need LEVEL 2 instruction to exist.

**Nothing in this package awards credit, and nothing in it demonstrates accreditation, state approval, or that any external institution will accept these courses.** Credit is issued by whoever issues the transcript.

## Credit recommendation

Using the common convention that **120 scheduled hours ≈ 1.0 credit** and **60 hours ≈ 0.5 credit**:

| | Per year | Four-year total | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **PE (recommended)** | **0.5 credit** | **2.0 credits** | ~85.5 scheduled hours per year |
| Health (recommended, see companion doc) | 0.25 credit | 1.0 credit | ~27 scheduled hours per year |
| **Combined** | **0.75 credit** | **3.0 credits** | against a Michigan Merit minimum of 1.0 credit total |

Two notes on the PE figure:

1. **0.5 credit per year is the conservative reading.** At ~85.5 scheduled hours, a transcript issuer using the 120-hour convention could defensibly record **0.75 credit per year (3.0 across four years)**. The recommendation stays at 0.5 because it is the figure that holds up under the least generous reading, and because Grades 11 and 12 already carry untimed independent training that is not counted in the hours.
2. **The LEVEL split is transcript-relevant.** Michigan's own structure treats LEVEL 2 as an advanced elective. A family that wants the transcript to reflect that can record Grades 9-10 as the required PE sequence and Grades 11-12 as **PE electives**, which is closer to how a Michigan district would label the same work.

## Participation floor and its effect on grading

Nothing in these courses scores body size, weight, weight change, body composition, or appearance, and no fitness-test norm table or percentile is used anywhere. Grades come from safe participation, skill cues, tactical and training reasoning, self-management, and revision — and **an adapted or described performance earns full credit, not partial credit.** A learner who completes four years entirely through seated, solo, low-impact, and described routes earns exactly the same credit as any other learner. That is a design commitment enforced by the `pe-inclusive-path` and `no-body-metrics` gates, not a case-by-case accommodation.
