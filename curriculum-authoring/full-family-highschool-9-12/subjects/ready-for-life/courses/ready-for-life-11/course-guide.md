# Manuel Academy — Ready for Life, Grade 11

**Postsecondary and Adult Systems.** Grade 11 plans the transition itself: pathways, applications and deadlines, health self-management, housing, and structured problem solving under real constraint.

- Course id: `ma-g11-ready-for-life`
- Units: 6  |  Lessons: 36  |  Schedule: 36 weeks x 1 day
- Competency framework: local Manuel Academy progression (`Manuel Academy RFL Grade 11 progression`). Ready for Life has no external standards body and none is claimed.

## Scope and sequence

### Unit 1 — Postsecondary Pathways

*Which postsecondary pathways are genuinely open to this learner, and what does each require?*

- Days: 6  |  Domains: RFL.POST
- Topics: college, trade, apprenticeship, military, and direct work; entry requirements and realistic timelines; cost and funding in outline, verified not assumed; fit: setting, structure, and support needs; talking to people on each pathway; building a shortlist that survives scrutiny
- Performance task: Compare four postsecondary pathways against this learner's goals, producing a documented shortlist with requirements and deadlines.

### Unit 2 — Applications, Deadlines, and Documentation

*What system makes sure nothing is missed when several deadlines overlap?*

- Days: 6  |  Domains: RFL.POST, RFL.PLAN
- Topics: building a deadline and document tracker; assembling transcripts, records, and references; writing a personal statement in the learner's own voice; proofreading and requesting feedback; submitting and confirming receipt; recovering when a deadline is missed
- Performance task: Build and run an application-tracking system across a full cycle of simulated and real deadlines with adult review.

### Unit 3 — Health and Wellness Self-Management

*What health responsibilities shift to the learner as they approach adulthood?*

- Days: 6  |  Domains: RFL.HEALTH
- Topics: making and preparing for an appointment; keeping personal health records privately; describing a concern accurately to a professional; routine care, prevention, and sleep; recognising when to seek help urgently; who to call and how, before it is needed
- Performance task: Build a personal health-management plan covering appointments, records, and help-seeking, without disclosing private detail.

### Unit 4 — Housing and Living Arrangements

*What does it actually take to live somewhere, and what is a tenant agreeing to?*

- Days: 6  |  Domains: RFL.ADULT, RFL.HOME
- Topics: comparing housing options on total cost and fit; reading a sample lease and naming its obligations; utilities, deposits, and setup sequence; roommates, shared costs, and written agreements; inspection, documentation, and moving out; verifying tenancy rules locally
- Performance task: Produce a simulated housing analysis: compare options, read a sample agreement, and build a move-in checklist and budget.

### Unit 5 — Problem Solving Under Constraint

*How does a person make a good decision when time, money, and information are all short?*

- Days: 6  |  Domains: RFL.SOLVE
- Topics: defining the actual problem before solving it; generating more than two options; weighing tradeoffs explicitly; deciding under incomplete information; building a fallback before committing; reviewing a decision after the outcome
- Performance task: Work a realistic multi-constraint problem to a defended decision, documenting the tradeoffs and the fallback.

### Unit 6 — Grade 11 Transition-Planning Capstone

*Does this learner have a credible, documented plan for what happens after high school?*

- Days: 6  |  Domains: RFL.POST, RFL.TRANS
- Topics: assembling the year's work into one plan; timeline with verified dates and requirements; naming support needs without shame; contingency planning for the primary pathway; identifying who helps with what; presenting the plan and revising it
- Performance task: Produce a documented postsecondary transition plan with pathway, requirements, timeline, funding outline, support needs, and contingencies, reviewed and attested by a guardian.
- This is the Grade 11 year capstone.

## Safety and dignity policies

**No shame.** Difficulty with a life skill is never treated as a character flaw. Honest self-assessment scores full marks. No learner is compared to another, and no result is framed as a personal or family failing.

**No unsafe unsupervised task.** Any task involving heat, sharp tools, appliances, chemicals, medication, electricity, heights, power tools, driving, water, money movement, or unfamiliar adults is guardian-supervised or simulated. Where supervision is unavailable, the simulated alternative carries **equal credit**.

**Guardian sign-off, and what a click cannot do.** A click cannot certify a real-world adult-supervised task. A learner-side completion toggle records only that the learner reports having finished. Evidence for this lesson is not complete until an adult attestation names the observing adult's role, what the adult actually observed, and the date.

Lessons requiring attestation in this course: day 4 and day 6 of every unit (12 of 36 lessons).

**No credential collection.** No password, account number, government identifier, card number, or real balance is requested, displayed, entered, or stored anywhere in this course.

**No forced private disclosure.** Autobiographical, household, financial, or health disclosure is never required. A fictional or analytical alternative is always available and never scored lower.

**Not advice.** Nothing here is medical, legal, financial, employment, or immigration advice. Local rules vary and are to be verified against a current local source rather than assumed.

**Mastery.** Practice generation never directly awards mastery. Do not mark mastery from one answer, and never from practice generation alone. Require accurate independent evidence plus the learner's own explanation on at least two separate occasions, at least one of them transferred to a situation the learner has not rehearsed. A guardian-attested real-world performance counts as one occasion only when the attestation is present.

**Guardian visibility.** Share the lesson target, completion state, evidence type, guardian attestation where required, and the next instructional step. Do not expose raw reflections, raw responses, recordings, photographs, household detail, health detail, or diagnosis-like language.
