## Unit 1: Concentration: Sustained Investigation

**Essential question:** What question is worth a year of work, and how do we keep answering it?

### Day 1 — Launch and diagnostic: identifying a driving artistic question
**Lesson ID:** `ma-g11-arts-and-music-u01-l01`  
**Standards:** Michigan Arts Education Standards: Visual Arts — Creating  
**Topics covered:** identifying a driving artistic question  
**Objective:** Surface what is already known about identifying a driving artistic question without penalty and establish the unit question.

**Student activity:** Learner records current thinking about identifying a driving artistic question — a prediction, a sketch, a first attempt, or a set of questions — before any instruction. Nothing here is scored for correctness.

**Exit ticket:** Name one thing you already understand about identifying a driving artistic question and one thing you want resolved by the end of the unit.

**Mastery rule:** Do not mark mastery from one answer. Use sketch or rehearsal record, created or performed work, artist statement, critique response, revision log, and portfolio reflection; require accurate independent evidence and successful transfer or retrieval on at least two occasions when feasible.
