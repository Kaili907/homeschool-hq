# Grade 8 → Grade 9 Handoff — Arts and Music

Entry point for the 9–12 progression is the existing Grade 8 course
`ma-g8-arts-and-music` (6 units, 72 sessions), which lives in the frozen
production release at
`curriculum-content/manuel-academy/1.0.0/grades/grade-8/courses/arts-and-music/`.

That course is **not modified by this work**. This file records what Grade 9
assumes the learner already did, and where each Grade 8 thread is picked up.

## Grade 8 exit state assumed by Grade 9

| Grade 8 unit | Exit capability carried into Grade 9 |
| --- | --- |
| 1 — Artistic Voice and Advanced Composition | Can develop an idea through sketching and revision and speak about personal artistic questions. |
| 2 — Design, Media, and Material Exploration | Has handled varied media safely and can iterate on a design brief. |
| 3 — Music Composition, Production, and Performance | Reads and writes basic notation and has created or produced a short original piece. |
| 4 — Theatre, Film, and Production Collaboration | Has rehearsed and presented in some format, including a private option. |
| 5 — Arts, Identity, Culture, and Ethics | Can discuss cultural context, attribution, and respectful representation. |
| 6 — Portfolio and Public or Private Exhibition | Has assembled a portfolio and written an artist statement. |

## How Grade 9 advances beyond Grade 8

Grade 9 builds above the Grade 8 exit point rather than restaging it. Grade 8
already covered advanced composition, symbol and metaphor, notation and digital
sequencing, recording and mixing, and directing, so Grade 9 starts above all of
those:

| Grade 9 unit | Grade 8 counterpart | What is new in Grade 9 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| U1 Advanced Studio Practice and Personal Direction | U1 artistic voice and advanced composition | Sustained series work toward a stated personal direction, with material experimentation and documented revision — not a return to elements and principles. |
| U2 Music Theory, Notation, and Analytical Listening | U3 composition, production, performance | Formal theory: modal scales, seventh chords, score reading and harmonic analysis of public-domain works, and ear-training transcription. Grade 8 stopped at notation and sequencing. |
| U3 Design Thinking and Applied Visual Communication | U2 design briefs and media | Typography systems, contrast and accessibility auditing, information and wayfinding design, and stakeholder-driven briefs. |
| U4 Theatre, Direction, and Presentation Craft | U4 theatre, film, production | Retains directing and blocking rather than dropping them, and adds script analysis and given circumstances. |
| U5 Critical Response, Aesthetics, and Historical Context | U5 identity, culture, ethics | Moves from discussion to sustained evidence-based critical writing and comparative analysis across time and place. |

Grade 10 U5 likewise advances beyond Grade 8 U5 rather than repeating it: it
adds artist rights, contracts, fair compensation, public funding, and ethical
material sourcing to the appropriation-and-attribution thread.

## Placement note

A learner who has not completed `ma-g8-arts-and-music` can still enter
`ma-g9-arts-and-music`, but Units 1 and 2 assume prior sketchbook practice and
the ability to read basic notation. No unit assumes a prior public performance
or a prior portfolio.

## Private-capable guarantee across all four years

No unit in Grades 9–12 requires a public performance, a recording of the
learner's voice, a photograph, or a video. Every performance, presentation,
exhibition, and capstone defense has a private, low-audience, or
written-and-scored route that carries the identical academic target. The Grade
12 capstone is substantial but fully completable in a private format.
