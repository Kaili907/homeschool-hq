# Grade 9 Arts and Music — Course Guide

**Course ID:** `ma-g9-arts-and-music`  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Instructional sessions:** 72  
**Typical session:** 45–75 minutes (60–90 in capstone units)  
**Cadence:** twice weekly

## Course description

A Grade 9 arts and music course that builds directly above the Grade 8 exit point: sustained studio practice toward a personal direction, formal music theory and transcription, applied visual communication, direction and presentation craft, and sustained evidence-based criticism.

## Course outcomes

By the end of the course, learners will:

1. Demonstrate the listed Michigan-aligned knowledge and practices through independent, accessible evidence.
2. Explain reasoning, cite evidence, document process, or justify design choices rather than supply unsupported answers.
3. Transfer learning to an unfamiliar task, text, situation, dataset, performance, or project.
4. Use feedback and error evidence to revise without shame or character judgments.
5. Complete the course capstone: **A Grade 9 portfolio with a sustained studio body of work, transcription and analysis evidence, an applied design solution, a sustained critical response, rights documentation, and a private-capable presentation.**

## Instructional model

Six-session units run: Launch and diagnostic; Explicit model; Guided practice; Application or project; Mastery check; Correction and reflection. Twelve-session units run: Launch and diagnostic; Concept model A; Guided practice A; Independent application A; Concept model B; Guided practice B; Investigation or close reading; Reteach and varied practice; Performance task build; Synthesis and review; Unit assessment; Correction and reflection.

Every topic in a unit receives at least one instructional lesson before any
assessment or correction lesson. Mastery-check, assessment, synthesis, and
correction lessons are scoped to the whole unit rather than to a single topic.
Lessons are resumable by segment, media is optional, and a readable fallback is
always specified.

## Mastery and grading

- One correct answer never establishes mastery.
- Unit assessments combine concept, representation or source use, application, error or claim analysis, connection, performance evidence, and reflection. Capstone units add a defense-and-response-to-critique prompt.
- Suggested reporting: **Secure**, **Developing**, or **Not Yet**, supported by evidence rather than a single percentage.
- Reassessment uses fresh items or a new application after targeted instruction.
- Approved breaks, accommodations, voice/no-voice choices, and alternate response modes are not failures.
- Parent summaries show target, completion, evidence type, and next step; they exclude raw private reflections, diagnosis language, and unnecessary personal data.

## Scope and sequence

| Unit | Title | Days | Standards anchors | Performance task |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Advanced Studio Practice and Personal Direction | 12 | Michigan Arts Education Standards: Visual Arts — Creating | Develop a studio series from a stated personal direction, with experimentation, revision, and process documentation. |
| 2 | Music Theory, Notation, and Analytical Listening | 12 | Michigan Arts Education Standards: Music — Creating; Music — Responding | Transcribe and analyse a public-domain or original passage, documenting harmonic function and an ear-training growth record. |
| 3 | Design Thinking and Applied Visual Communication | 12 | Michigan Arts Education Standards: Visual Arts — Creating; Visual Arts — Connecting | Complete an accessible design solution to a stated brief with prototype iterations, user feedback, and a contrast audit. |
| 4 | Theatre, Direction, and Presentation Craft | 12 | Michigan Arts Education Standards: Theatre — Creating; Theatre — Performing | Direct and present a rehearsed scene in a chosen private or shared format with a director’s book and an annotated rehearsal record. |
| 5 | Critical Response, Aesthetics, and Historical Context | 12 | Michigan Arts Education Standards: Visual Arts — Responding; Music — Connecting | Write a sustained evidence-based critical response comparing two works across historical and cultural context. |
| 6 | Foundations Portfolio and Private-Capable Presentation | 12 | Michigan Arts Education Standards: Visual Arts — Presenting; Visual Arts — Responding | Assemble a foundations portfolio with an artist statement, documentation, a rights record, and a chosen private or public presentation mode. |

## Capstone

A Grade 9 portfolio with a sustained studio body of work, transcription and analysis evidence, an applied design solution, a sustained critical response, rights documentation, and a private-capable presentation.

The capstone may be presented publicly, privately, in writing, orally, through a demonstration, or in another accessible format that preserves the learning targets.
