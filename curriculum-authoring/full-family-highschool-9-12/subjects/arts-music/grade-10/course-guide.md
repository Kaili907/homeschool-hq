# Grade 10 Arts and Music — Course Guide

**Course ID:** `ma-g10-arts-and-music`  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Instructional sessions:** 72  
**Typical session:** 45–75 minutes (60–90 in capstone units)  
**Cadence:** twice weekly

## Course description

A Grade 10 arts and music course deepening material technique, harmonic composition and arranging, time-based media production, disciplined rehearsal craft with private alternatives, and the ethics and economics of artistic practice.

## Course outcomes

By the end of the course, learners will:

1. Demonstrate the listed Michigan-aligned knowledge and practices through independent, accessible evidence.
2. Explain reasoning, cite evidence, document process, or justify design choices rather than supply unsupported answers.
3. Transfer learning to an unfamiliar task, text, situation, dataset, performance, or project.
4. Use feedback and error evidence to revise without shame or character judgments.
5. Complete the course capstone: **A Grade 10 portfolio with advanced studio, composition, and media evidence, a refined artist statement, an ethics case study, a rights audit, and a private-capable presentation.**

## Instructional model

Six-session units run: Launch and diagnostic; Explicit model; Guided practice; Application or project; Mastery check; Correction and reflection. Twelve-session units run: Launch and diagnostic; Concept model A; Guided practice A; Independent application A; Concept model B; Guided practice B; Investigation or close reading; Reteach and varied practice; Performance task build; Synthesis and review; Unit assessment; Correction and reflection.

Every topic in a unit receives at least one instructional lesson before any
assessment or correction lesson. Mastery-check, assessment, synthesis, and
correction lessons are scoped to the whole unit rather than to a single topic.
Lessons are resumable by segment, media is optional, and a readable fallback is
always specified.

## Mastery and grading

- One correct answer never establishes mastery.
- Unit assessments combine concept, representation or source use, application, error or claim analysis, connection, performance evidence, and reflection. Capstone units add a defense-and-response-to-critique prompt.
- Suggested reporting: **Secure**, **Developing**, or **Not Yet**, supported by evidence rather than a single percentage.
- Reassessment uses fresh items or a new application after targeted instruction.
- Approved breaks, accommodations, voice/no-voice choices, and alternate response modes are not failures.
- Parent summaries show target, completion, evidence type, and next step; they exclude raw private reflections, diagnosis language, and unnecessary personal data.

## Scope and sequence

| Unit | Title | Days | Standards anchors | Performance task |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Advanced Studio Technique and Materials | 12 | Michigan Arts Education Standards: Visual Arts — Creating | Produce a technically ambitious studio work with a documented technique study and a safety plan. |
| 2 | Music Theory, Composition, and Arranging | 12 | Michigan Arts Education Standards: Music — Creating | Compose and notate an original short work with harmonic analysis and an arranging rationale. |
| 3 | Digital Media Arts, Audio, and Production | 12 | Michigan Arts Education Standards: Media Arts — Creating; Media Arts — Producing | Produce a time-based or audio media piece using original or properly licensed assets with a provenance log. |
| 4 | Performance, Presentation, and Production Craft | 12 | Michigan Arts Education Standards: Music — Performing; Theatre — Performing | Deliver a prepared performance or production contribution in a chosen private or shared format with a rehearsal log. |
| 5 | Cultural Ownership, Ethics, and the Economics of Practice | 12 | Michigan Arts Education Standards: Visual Arts — Connecting; Music — Connecting | Research and present an ethics case study on artistic practice, in writing or in a chosen private or shared format, with sourced evidence and a defended position. |
| 6 | Portfolio, Artist Statement, and Critical Review | 12 | Michigan Arts Education Standards: Visual Arts — Presenting; Visual Arts — Responding | Curate a sophomore portfolio with revision evidence, a refined statement, and a completed rights and permissions audit. |

## Capstone

A Grade 10 portfolio with advanced studio, composition, and media evidence, a refined artist statement, an ethics case study, a rights audit, and a private-capable presentation.

The capstone may be presented publicly, privately, in writing, orally, through a demonstration, or in another accessible format that preserves the learning targets.
