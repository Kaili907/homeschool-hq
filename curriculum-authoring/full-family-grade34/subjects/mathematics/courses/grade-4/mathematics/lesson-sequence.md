## Unit 1: Mathematical Habits and Place Value to One Million

**Essential question:** How does the base-ten structure let us read, compare, and round whole numbers of any size up to one million?  
**Standards:** 4.NBT.1, 4.NBT.2, 4.NBT.3, MP.1, MP.3, MP.4, MP.6, MP.7  
**Fluency target:** Read, write, compare, and round multi-digit whole numbers through the hundred thousands.  
**Performance task:** Build a Ten Times Bigger explainer showing place-value relationships with models, comparisons, and a rounding decision made for a stated purpose.

### Day 1 (course day 1) - Launch and diagnostic: making sense of large numbers and unfamiliar problems
**Lesson ID:** `ma-g4-mathematics-u01-l01`  
**Standards:** MP.1, MP.3  
**Evidence type:** instruction
