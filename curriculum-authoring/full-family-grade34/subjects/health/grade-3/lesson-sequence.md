# Grade 3 Health — Complete Lesson Sequence

**Sessions:** 36
**Schedule:** 36 weeks x 1 session per week

## Unit 1: Healthy Bodies, Healthy Routines

**Unit ID:** `ma-g3-health-u01`
**Standards:** Michigan Health Practice 5: Self-Management and Goal Setting; Michigan Health Practice 3: Information and Resource Seeking; Michigan Health Topic (Grades 3-5): Personal Health and Wellness
**Essential question:** How do everyday routines keep my body well and stop germs from spreading?
**Performance task:** Build a personal daily health routine card with morning, daytime, and bedtime steps, and explain what each step protects. No body measurement is used.
**Adapted alternative:** Any routine step may be described, drawn, sequenced with picture cards, or demonstrated by an adult instead of written. A step a learner cannot do independently is planned as an adult-supported step, which counts fully.

### Session 1 (Unit day 1) — Launch and diagnostic: daily hygiene routines
**Lesson ID:** `ma-g3-health-u01-l01`
**Phase:** Launch and diagnostic
**Focus:** daily hygiene routines
**Objective:** Surface what the learner already knows about daily hygiene routines, without penalty, and establish the unit question.

**Student activity:** The learner applies what they learned about daily hygiene routines to a new fictional situation and records the choice plus the reason behind it.

**Exit ticket:** Name one thing you already knew about daily hygiene routines and one question you now have.

### Session 2 (Unit day 2) — Explicit model: handwashing and how germs spread
**Lesson ID:** `ma-g3-health-u01-l02`
**Phase:** Explicit model
**Focus:** handwashing and how germs spread
**Objective:** Build an accurate mental model of handwashing and how germs spread from an explicit demonstration, worked scenario, or age-appropriate text.

**Student activity:** The learner applies what they learned about handwashing and how germs spread to a new fictional situation and records the choice plus the reason behind it.

**Exit ticket:** State the most important rule or fact about handwashing and how germs spread in your own words.

### Session 3 (Unit day 3) — Guided practice: dental care
**Lesson ID:** `ma-g3-health-u01-l03`
**Phase:** Guided practice
**Focus:** dental care
**Objective:** Practice dental care with prompts, feedback, and gradually reduced support.

**Student activity:** The learner applies what they learned about dental care to a new fictional situation and records the choice plus the reason behind it.

**Exit ticket:** Using the practice situation, say what the safest or healthiest next step is and why.

### Session 4 (Unit day 4) — Application or project: sleep and rest
**Lesson ID:** `ma-g3-health-u01-l04`
**Phase:** Application or project
**Focus:** sleep and rest
**Objective:** Apply sleep and rest to the unit performance task and justify the choices made.

**Student activity:** The learner applies what they learned about sleep and rest to a new fictional situation and records the choice plus the reason behind it.

**Exit ticket:** Show one part of your project that uses sleep and rest and say why you made that choice.

### Session 5 (Unit day 5) — Mastery check: sun and skin care
**Lesson ID:** `ma-g3-health-u01-l05`
**Phase:** Mastery check
**Focus:** sun and skin care
**Objective:** Demonstrate current understanding of sun and skin care through scenario response and applied evidence.

**Student activity:** The learner applies what they learned about sun and skin care to a new fictional situation and records the choice plus the reason behind it.

**Exit ticket:** Explain sun and skin care to someone who has not learned it yet, using one example.

### Session 6 (Unit day 6) — Correction and reflection: resting and recovering when sick
**Lesson ID:** `ma-g3-health-u01-l06`
**Phase:** Correction and reflection
**Focus:** resting and recovering when sick
**Objective:** Teach and consolidate resting and recovering when sick, correct any errors from the unit, explain the corrections, and identify where the unit transfers.

**Student activity:** The learner applies what they learned about resting and recovering when sick to a new fictional situation and records the choice plus the reason behind it.

**Exit ticket:** Name one thing you corrected or would do differently, and one other place resting and recovering when sick would be useful.

**Unit assessment:** `ma-g3-health-u01-assessment`

## Unit 2: Food, Water, and Moving My Body

**Unit ID:** `ma-g3-health-u02`
**Standards:** Michigan Health Practice 1: Self-Awareness and Analyzing Influences; Michigan Health Practice 5: Self-Management and Goal Setting; Michigan Health Topic (Grades 3-5): Balanced Eating and Physical Activity
**Essential question:** How do food, water, and movement give my body energy for the things I want to do?
**Performance task:** Design flexible fuel-and-move plans for three different kinds of days, including a safe plan for sharing food with someone who has an allergy. No calories, weights, diets, body-size targets, or good-and-bad food labels are used.
**Adapted alternative:** Plans may use pictures, cut-outs, dictation, or spoken description. Learners with food allergies, medical diets, religious or cultural food practices, or limited food access plan with the foods actually available to them, and the adult may substitute any example.

## Unit 5: Staying Safe at Home, Outside, and Online

**Unit ID:** `ma-g3-health-u05`

**Essential question:** How do I spot a hazard early and choose the safest next step?
**Performance task:** Make a household and outdoor safety plan with hazard checks, safe steps, an escape route, and who to contact. A guardian reviews and confirms the plan.
**Adapted alternative:** A safety walk may be done by an adult with the learner directing from a seated position, by spoken description with no photographs taken, or entirely through a floor-plan drawing. No learner is required to reach, lift, or test any hazard.
