# Grade 4 Arts and Music — Course Guide

**Course ID:** `ma-g4-arts-music`
**Version:** 1.0.0
**Instructional sessions:** 72
**Typical session:** 40–60
**Cadence:** twice weekly

## Course description

An integrated arts course developing creation, presentation, response, technique, and respectful cultural context across visual art, music, theater, and dance.

## Course outcomes

By the end of the course, learners will:

1. Demonstrate the listed Michigan-aligned knowledge and practices through independent, accessible evidence.
2. Explain reasoning, cite evidence, document process, or justify design choices rather than supply unsupported answers.
3. Transfer learning to an unfamiliar task, text, situation, dataset, performance, or project.
4. Use feedback and error evidence to revise without shame or character judgments.
5. Complete the course capstone: **A choice-based arts portfolio and exhibition or private sharing event.**

## Instructional model

Each unit follows a recurring arc: low-stakes launch and diagnostic; explicit modeling; guided practice; independent application; investigation or close reading; performance-task planning and creation; synthesis; unit assessment; targeted correction; and reflection or publication. Lessons are resumable by segment, media is optional, and a readable fallback is always specified.

## Mastery and grading

- One correct answer never establishes mastery.
- Unit assessments combine concept, representation/source use, application, error or claim analysis, connection, performance evidence, and reflection.
- Suggested reporting: **Secure**, **Developing**, or **Not Yet**, supported by evidence rather than a single percentage.
- Reassessment uses fresh items or a new application after targeted instruction.
- Approved breaks, accommodations, voice/no-voice choices, and alternate response modes are not failures.
- Parent summaries show target, completion, evidence type, and next step; they exclude raw private reflections, diagnosis language, and unnecessary personal data.

## Scope and sequence

| Unit | Title | Days | Standards anchors | Performance task |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Elements and Principles of Art and Music | 12 | Michigan Arts: Visual Arts – Creating, Michigan Arts: Visual Arts – Responding, Michigan Arts: Music – Performing, Michigan Arts: Music – Responding | Create an artwork and a short rhythmic or melodic piece that each show purposeful use of one element or principle. |
| 2 | Techniques, Media, and Craftsmanship | 12 | Michigan Arts: Visual Arts – Creating, Michigan Arts: Visual Arts – Presenting | Design a small collection around a shared theme using at least two media. |
| 3 | Music Foundations and Listening | 12 | Michigan Arts: Music – Performing, Michigan Arts: Music – Responding | Create an annotated listening map and perform or sequence a short rhythmic or melodic piece, with a private or recorded option. |
| 4 | Theater, Storytelling, and Movement | 12 | Michigan Arts: Theatre – Creating, Michigan Arts: Theatre – Performing, Michigan Arts: Dance – Creating | Develop an original short scene, readers theater, or movement story with accessible, private, or small-audience performance options. |
| 5 | Arts, Culture, and Context | 12 | Michigan Arts: Connecting, Michigan Arts: Responding | Curate a small context-rich exhibit or playlist using permitted images, audio, descriptions, or learner-created work. |
| 6 | Portfolio, Rehearsal, and Exhibition | 12 | Michigan Arts: Creating, Michigan Arts: Performing/Presenting, Michigan Arts: Responding, Michigan Arts: Connecting | Present a visual, musical, theatrical, dance, or mixed-media portfolio with process evidence, privately or publicly by choice. |
