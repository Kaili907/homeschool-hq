# Arts and Music — Portfolio and Presentation Tasks (Grades 3–4)

Every unit ends with a performance task (see each unit's `performance_task` in `units.json` and the
`"Performance task build"` lesson phase) that becomes one artifact in the learner's arts portfolio. No
performance task requires public performance, a voice recording, or a photo/camera; every task states a
private, written, or no-audio alternative.

## Grade 3 (`ma-g3-arts-music`) unit projects

| Unit | Project |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | A small artwork and a short rhythm pattern, each using two elements explored this unit |
| 2 | A small collection of work using at least two different materials or techniques |
| 3 | A short rhythmic pattern performed or sequenced with voice, body percussion, or an instrument of choice — private or recorded option available |
| 4 | An original short movement story or scene, shared privately or with a small audience |
| 5 | A small collection connecting a personal experience to one visual and one musical example |
| 6 | A portfolio of favorite work from the year, shared privately, in writing, or with an audience of choice |

**Capstone:** a choice-based art and music portfolio shared privately, in writing, or with an audience of
choice — never a required public performance.

## Grade 4 (`ma-g4-arts-music`) unit projects

| Unit | Project |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | An artwork and a short rhythmic or melodic piece, each showing purposeful use of one element or principle |
| 2 | A small collection around a shared theme using at least two media |
| 3 | An annotated listening map plus a performed or sequenced rhythmic/melodic piece — private or recorded option available |
| 4 | An original short scene, readers theater, or movement story with accessible, private, or small-audience performance options |
| 5 | A small context-rich exhibit or playlist using permitted images, audio, descriptions, or learner-created work |
| 6 | A visual, musical, theatrical, dance, or mixed-media portfolio with process evidence, private or public by choice |

**Capstone:** a choice-based arts portfolio and exhibition or private sharing event.

## Portfolio evidence and grading

Each project is scored with the shared assessment rubric in the unit's `assessments.json` entry
(`rubric_dimensions`: accuracy or fidelity; evidence and reasoning; application or performance; checking
and revision) plus the `performance evidence` prompt, which asks the learner to present the strongest
evidence from the unit project directly. Guardians see the target, completion state, evidence type, and
next step for each project — not raw answers, voice recordings, or private reflections. The final piece in
each portfolio must remain the learner's own authorship; only original student work, public-domain
material, or properly licensed excerpts may be used.
