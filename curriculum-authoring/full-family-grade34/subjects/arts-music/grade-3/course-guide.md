# Grade 3 Arts and Music — Course Guide

**Course ID:** `ma-g3-arts-music`
**Version:** 1.0.0
**Instructional sessions:** 72
**Typical session:** 40–60
**Cadence:** twice weekly

## Course description

An integrated arts course introducing creation, response, connection, and choice-based presentation across visual art, music, theater, and movement.

## Course outcomes

By the end of the course, learners will:

1. Demonstrate the listed Michigan-aligned knowledge and practices through independent, accessible evidence.
2. Explain reasoning, cite evidence, document process, or justify design choices rather than supply unsupported answers.
3. Transfer learning to an unfamiliar task, text, situation, dataset, performance, or project.
4. Use feedback and error evidence to revise without shame or character judgments.
5. Complete the course capstone: **A choice-based art and music portfolio shared privately, in writing, or with an audience of choice.**

## Instructional model

Each unit follows a recurring arc: low-stakes launch and diagnostic; explicit modeling; guided practice; independent application; investigation or close reading; performance-task planning and creation; synthesis; unit assessment; targeted correction; and reflection or publication. Lessons are resumable by segment, media is optional, and a readable fallback is always specified.

## Mastery and grading

- One correct answer never establishes mastery.
- Unit assessments combine concept, representation/source use, application, error or claim analysis, connection, performance evidence, and reflection.
- Suggested reporting: **Secure**, **Developing**, or **Not Yet**, supported by evidence rather than a single percentage.
- Reassessment uses fresh items or a new application after targeted instruction.
- Approved breaks, accommodations, voice/no-voice choices, and alternate response modes are not failures.
- Parent summaries show target, completion, evidence type, and next step; they exclude raw private reflections, diagnosis language, and unnecessary personal data.

## Scope and sequence

| Unit | Title | Days | Standards anchors | Performance task |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Looking and Listening: Elements of Art and Sound | 12 | Michigan Arts: Visual Arts – Creating, Michigan Arts: Music – Responding | Create a small artwork and a short rhythm pattern that each use two elements explored this unit. |
| 2 | Materials and Making | 12 | Michigan Arts: Visual Arts – Creating, Michigan Arts: Visual Arts – Presenting | Create a small collection of work using at least two different materials or techniques. |
| 3 | Music Foundations: Beat, Voice, and Instruments | 12 | Michigan Arts: Music – Performing, Michigan Arts: Music – Responding | Perform or sequence a short rhythmic pattern using voice, body percussion, or an instrument of choice, with a private or recorded option. |
| 4 | Stories in Motion: Drama and Movement | 12 | Michigan Arts: Theatre – Creating, Michigan Arts: Dance – Creating | Create and share, privately or with a small audience, a short original movement story or scene. |
| 5 | Art and Music Around Us | 12 | Michigan Arts: Connecting, Michigan Arts: Responding | Create a small collection connecting a personal experience to one visual and one musical example. |
| 6 | Sharing My Work: Portfolio and Choice Showcase | 12 | Michigan Arts: Creating, Michigan Arts: Performing/Presenting, Michigan Arts: Responding, Michigan Arts: Connecting | Assemble and share, privately, in writing, or with an audience of choice, a portfolio of favorite work from the year with short reflections. |
