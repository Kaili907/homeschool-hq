## Unit 1: Readers and Writers Begin: Habits, Print, and Sentences

*What do careful readers and writers do every day, and how do I show what a text actually says?*

### Day 1 (unit day 1) — Launch and baseline: what strong readers and writers do, and a no-penalty baseline
**Lesson ID:** `ma-g3-english-language-arts-u01-l01`  
**Standards:** 3.RL.1, 3.RI.1, 3.SL.1  
**Evidence:** guided  
**Text:** The Bus-Stop Library (original)  
**Objective:** Surface what you already know and produce a no-penalty baseline.
