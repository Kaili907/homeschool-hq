# Grade 8 Arts and Music — Course Guide

**Course ID:** `ma-g8-arts-and-music`  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Instructional sessions:** 72  
**Typical session:** 40–60  
**Cadence:** twice weekly

## Course description

An integrated Grade 8 arts program focused on artistic voice, design and media, music, theatre/film, cultural ethics, collaboration, and high-school-ready portfolio presentation.

## Course outcomes

By the end of the course, learners will:

1. Demonstrate the listed Michigan-aligned knowledge and practices through independent, accessible evidence.
2. Explain reasoning, cite evidence, document process, or justify design choices rather than supply unsupported answers.
3. Transfer learning to an unfamiliar task, text, situation, dataset, performance, or project.
4. Use feedback and error evidence to revise without shame or character judgments.
5. Complete the course capstone: **A polished cross-arts portfolio and public-or-private capstone presentation.**

## Instructional model

Each unit follows a recurring arc: low-stakes launch and diagnostic; explicit modeling; guided practice; independent application; investigation or close reading; performance-task planning and creation; synthesis; unit assessment; targeted correction; and reflection or publication. Lessons are resumable by segment, media is optional, and a readable fallback is always specified.

## Mastery and grading

- One correct answer never establishes mastery.
- Unit assessments combine concept, representation/source use, application, error or claim analysis, connection, performance evidence, and reflection.
- Suggested reporting: **Secure**, **Developing**, or **Not Yet**, supported by evidence rather than a single percentage.
- Reassessment uses fresh items or a new application after targeted instruction.
- Approved breaks, accommodations, voice/no-voice choices, and alternate response modes are not failures.
- Parent summaries show target, completion, evidence type, and next step; they exclude raw private reflections, diagnosis language, and unnecessary personal data.

## Scope and sequence

| Unit | Title | Days | Standards anchors | Performance task |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Artistic Voice and Advanced Composition | 12 | Michigan Arts: Visual Arts Create, Respond | Develop a coherent visual series with an artist statement and process evidence. |
| 2 | Design, Media, and Material Exploration | 12 | Michigan Arts: Visual Arts Create, Media Arts Create | Solve a communication or environment design problem through multiple prototypes. |
| 3 | Music Composition, Production, and Performance | 12 | Michigan Arts: Music Create, Perform, Respond | Compose or arrange an original short work and present it live, recorded, sequenced, or through a detailed score/listening map. |
| 4 | Theatre, Film, and Production Collaboration | 12 | Michigan Arts: Theatre Create, Perform, Media Arts Produce | Produce a short scene, audio drama, film plan, or staged reading with clear roles and permissions. |
| 5 | Arts, Identity, Culture, and Ethics | 12 | Michigan Arts: Connect, Respond | Curate an ethical context-rich exhibition or program note set around a question of art and society. |
| 6 | Portfolio and Public or Private Exhibition | 12 | Michigan Arts: Present/Perform, Respond, Connect | Complete a polished cross-arts portfolio and exhibition, performance, screening, or private defense. |
