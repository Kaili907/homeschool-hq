# Grade 8 Mathematics — Course Guide

**Course ID:** `ma-g8-mathematics`  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Instructional sessions:** 180  
**Typical session:** 45–60  
**Cadence:** daily

## Course description

A complete Grade 8 mathematics course emphasizing real numbers, exponents, linear equations, functions, systems, transformations, geometry, bivariate data, and modeling.

## Course outcomes

By the end of the course, learners will:

1. Demonstrate the listed Michigan-aligned knowledge and practices through independent, accessible evidence.
2. Explain reasoning, cite evidence, document process, or justify design choices rather than supply unsupported answers.
3. Transfer learning to an unfamiliar task, text, situation, dataset, performance, or project.
4. Use feedback and error evidence to revise without shame or character judgments.
5. Complete the course capstone: **An algebraic modeling capstone with data, equations or functions, geometry, assumptions, verification, and presentation.**

## Instructional model

Each unit follows a recurring arc: low-stakes launch and diagnostic; explicit modeling; guided practice; independent application; investigation or close reading; performance-task planning and creation; synthesis; unit assessment; targeted correction; and reflection or publication. Lessons are resumable by segment, media is optional, and a readable fallback is always specified.

## Mastery and grading

- One correct answer never establishes mastery.
- Unit assessments combine concept, representation/source use, application, error or claim analysis, connection, performance evidence, and reflection.
- Suggested reporting: **Secure**, **Developing**, or **Not Yet**, supported by evidence rather than a single percentage.
- Reassessment uses fresh items or a new application after targeted instruction.
- Approved breaks, accommodations, voice/no-voice choices, and alternate response modes are not failures.
- Parent summaries show target, completion, evidence type, and next step; they exclude raw private reflections, diagnosis language, and unnecessary personal data.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Real Numbers and Irrational Numbers | 18 | 8.NS.1, 8.NS.2, MP.2, MP.6 | Create a real-number field guide with approximations, representations, and error bounds. |
| 2 | Integer Exponents and Scientific Notation | 18 | 8.EE.1, 8.EE.3, 8.EE.4 | Analyze a dataset spanning very large and very small quantities and communicate it accurately. |
| 3 | Linear Equations | 18 | 8.EE.7 | Build and defend linear-equation models for several real or simulated decisions. |
| 4 | Slope, Lines, and Proportional Connections | 18 | 8.EE.5, 8.EE.6 | Model and compare two linear situations using graphs, equations, units, and assumptions. |
| 5 | Functions | 18 | 8.F.1, 8.F.2, 8.F.3, 8.F.4, 8.F.5 | Create a function storybook or dashboard comparing multiple representations and behaviors. |
| 6 | Systems of Linear Equations | 18 | 8.EE.8 | Use a system to compare two plans, rates, or constraints and write a recommendation. |
| 7 | Transformations, Congruence, and Similarity | 18 | 8.G.1, 8.G.2, 8.G.3, 8.G.4, 8.G.5 | Create a transformation-based design and prove selected figures congruent or similar. |
| 8 | Pythagorean Theorem and Coordinate Geometry | 18 | 8.G.6, 8.G.7, 8.G.8 | Solve and document a route, accessibility, construction, or design problem using the Pythagorean theorem. |
