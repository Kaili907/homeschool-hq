# Manuel Academy — Grade 8 Complete Curriculum

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Year model:** 36 weeks  
**Total lesson blueprints:** 936

## Program design

Grade 8 includes ten coordinated courses. Mathematics and English Language Arts meet daily; science, social studies, and physical education meet three times weekly; arts meets twice weekly; health, Ready for Life, and technology meet weekly; financial literacy meets twice weekly. The schedule is a planning default, not a restriction on flexible pacing.

## Courses

| Course | Lessons | Units | Cadence | Capstone |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mathematics | 180 | 10 | daily | An algebraic modeling capstone with data, equations or functions, geometry, assumptions, verification, and presentation. |
| English Language Arts | 180 | 10 | daily | A curated transition portfolio and formal defense with private presentation options. |
| Science | 108 | 9 | three times weekly | A research or engineering defense using models, data, tradeoffs, revisions, and clear limitations. |
| Social Studies | 108 | 9 | three times weekly | A constitutional-history inquiry exhibition and civic reasoning project using primary and secondary evidence. |
| Health | 36 | 6 | weekly | A fact-checked health communication and self-advocacy portfolio with protected privacy. |
| Physical Education | 108 | 9 | three times weekly | A lifelong-movement transition plan, portfolio defense, and learner-facilitated inclusive event. |
| Ready for Life | 36 | 6 | weekly | A one-month independent-living simulation reviewed for safety, feasibility, communication, and backup planning. |
| Technology and Computer Science | 36 | 6 | weekly | A documented technology capstone with source, code or logic, testing, accessibility, privacy, security, and impact evidence. |
| Arts and Music | 72 | 6 | twice weekly | A polished cross-arts portfolio and public-or-private capstone presentation. |
| Financial Literacy | 72 | 7 | weekly or twice weekly | A comprehensive one-year fictional financial plan and oral or written defense. |

## Evidence and mastery

Progress is based on knowledge, reasoning, application, creation, and retrieval over time. Time spent alone is not mastery. A learner may receive reteaching, a different representation, fewer items, a break, or an alternate response format while the academic target remains intact.

## Family use

- Use `daily-schedule.csv` for a ready-to-run 180-day calendar.
- Use `family-weekly-overview.md` for unit-level planning.
- Open each course folder for the teacher guide, unit plan, assessments, JSONL lesson records, and readable lesson sequence.
- Preserve private learner reflections and do not require photographs, voice recordings, precise location, or account credentials.
