# Manuel Academy — Grade 5 Complete Curriculum

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Year model:** 36 weeks  
**Total lesson blueprints:** 900

## Program design

Grade 5 includes ten coordinated courses. Mathematics and English Language Arts meet daily; science, social studies, and physical education meet three times weekly; arts meets twice weekly; health, Ready for Life, and technology meet weekly; financial literacy meets weekly. The schedule is a planning default, not a restriction on flexible pacing.

## Courses

| Course | Lessons | Units | Cadence | Capstone |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mathematics | 180 | 10 | daily | Integrated planning capstone with a model, calculations, error checks, written reasoning, and presentation. |
| English Language Arts | 180 | 10 | daily | A curated literacy portfolio and public-or-private presentation demonstrating growth across genres. |
| Science | 108 | 9 | three times weekly | A systems investigation and engineering-design exhibition with text-only and no-media alternatives. |
| Social Studies | 108 | 9 | three times weekly | A museum-style inquiry exhibition connecting historical evidence to responsible civic participation. |
| Health | 36 | 6 | weekly | A private-safe personal wellness and safety toolkit; no private disclosure or body measurement required. |
| Physical Education | 108 | 9 | three times weekly | A personal movement portfolio and learner-designed inclusive activity festival. |
| Ready for Life | 36 | 6 | weekly | A guardian-visible “Finish the Loop” contribution project with checklist, evidence, and reflection. |
| Technology and Computer Science | 36 | 6 | weekly | A user-centered prototype with algorithm, test log, accessibility check, and presentation. |
| Arts and Music | 72 | 6 | twice weekly | A choice-based arts portfolio and exhibition or private sharing event. |
| Financial Literacy | 36 | 6 | weekly or twice weekly | A simulated marketplace and personal money-choice portfolio; no real financial accounts or purchases required. |

## Evidence and mastery

Progress is based on knowledge, reasoning, application, creation, and retrieval over time. Time spent alone is not mastery. A learner may receive reteaching, a different representation, fewer items, a break, or an alternate response format while the academic target remains intact.

## Family use

- Use `daily-schedule.csv` for a ready-to-run 180-day calendar.
- Use `family-weekly-overview.md` for unit-level planning.
- Open each course folder for the teacher guide, unit plan, assessments, JSONL lesson records, and readable lesson sequence.
- Preserve private learner reflections and do not require photographs, voice recordings, precise location, or account credentials.
