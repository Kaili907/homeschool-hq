# Grade 5 Physical Education — Course Guide

**Course ID:** `ma-g5-physical-education`  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Instructional sessions:** 108  
**Typical session:** 35–60  
**Cadence:** three times weekly

## Course description

A varied, inclusive physical-education course that develops motor skills, knowledge, strategy, responsible behavior, and personally meaningful lifelong activity.

## Course outcomes

By the end of the course, learners will:

1. Demonstrate the listed Michigan-aligned knowledge and practices through independent, accessible evidence.
2. Explain reasoning, cite evidence, document process, or justify design choices rather than supply unsupported answers.
3. Transfer learning to an unfamiliar task, text, situation, dataset, performance, or project.
4. Use feedback and error evidence to revise without shame or character judgments.
5. Complete the course capstone: **A personal movement portfolio and learner-designed inclusive activity festival.**

## Instructional model

Each unit follows a recurring arc: low-stakes launch and diagnostic; explicit modeling; guided practice; independent application; investigation or close reading; performance-task planning and creation; synthesis; unit assessment; targeted correction; and reflection or publication. Lessons are resumable by segment, media is optional, and a readable fallback is always specified.

## Mastery and grading

- One correct answer never establishes mastery.
- Unit assessments combine concept, representation/source use, application, error or claim analysis, connection, performance evidence, and reflection.
- Suggested reporting: **Secure**, **Developing**, or **Not Yet**, supported by evidence rather than a single percentage.
- Reassessment uses fresh items or a new application after targeted instruction.
- Approved breaks, accommodations, voice/no-voice choices, and alternate response modes are not failures.
- Parent summaries show target, completion, evidence type, and next step; they exclude raw private reflections, diagnosis language, and unnecessary personal data.

## Scope and sequence

| Unit | Title | Days | Standards anchors | Performance task |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Movement Foundations and Safe Participation | 12 | Michigan PE Standards 1, 2, 4 | Create and teach a safe movement circuit with adjustable challenge levels. |
| 2 | Fitness Concepts for Health and Enjoyment | 12 | Michigan PE Standards 3, 4, 5 | Build a balanced two-week activity plan based on enjoyment, safety, recovery, and individual progress. |
| 3 | Throwing, Catching, Striking, and Kicking | 12 | Michigan PE Standards 1, 2 | Complete a skill-challenge portfolio showing cue use and personal improvement. |
| 4 | Games, Strategy, and Fair Play | 12 | Michigan PE Standards 2, 4, 5 | Design an inclusive small-sided game with clear rules, safety checks, and strategy reflection. |
| 5 | Rhythm, Dance, and Creative Movement | 12 | Michigan PE Standards 1, 2, 5 | Create or learn a short sequence and explain its structure or cultural context. |
| 6 | Outdoor and Lifetime Activities | 12 | Michigan PE Standards 1, 3, 5 | Plan a safe, accessible outdoor activity with route, equipment, weather, and contingency checks. |
| 7 | Individual Skill Development | 12 | Michigan PE Standards 1, 2, 3 | Choose one individual skill and document deliberate practice without comparison-based scoring. |
| 8 | Teamwork, Leadership, and Conflict Repair | 12 | Michigan PE Standards 4, 5 | Lead one safe activity segment and reflect on communication, inclusion, and repair. |
| 9 | Personal Activity Portfolio and Celebration | 12 | Michigan PE Standards 3, 4, 5 | Present a personal activity portfolio focused on skill, confidence, enjoyment, safety, and next steps. |
