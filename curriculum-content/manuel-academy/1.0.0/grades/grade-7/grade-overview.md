# Manuel Academy — Grade 7 Complete Curriculum

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Year model:** 36 weeks  
**Total lesson blueprints:** 900

## Program design

Grade 7 includes ten coordinated courses. Mathematics and English Language Arts meet daily; science, social studies, and physical education meet three times weekly; arts meets twice weekly; health, Ready for Life, and technology meet weekly; financial literacy meets weekly. The schedule is a planning default, not a restriction on flexible pacing.

## Courses

| Course | Lessons | Units | Cadence | Capstone |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mathematics | 180 | 10 | daily | A multi-domain modeling capstone with calculations, representations, limitations, and oral or written defense. |
| English Language Arts | 180 | 10 | daily | A portfolio and inquiry-based communication demonstrating independent reading, writing, source, and presentation skills. |
| Science | 108 | 9 | three times weekly | An engineering and evidence exhibition linking a tested design to a scientific model. |
| Social Studies | 108 | 9 | three times weekly | A comparative world-history inquiry exhibition using maps, sources, argument, and a civic reasoning connection. |
| Health | 36 | 6 | weekly | A private-safe wellness, communication, and safety portfolio grounded in reliable information and trusted support. |
| Physical Education | 108 | 9 | three times weekly | A personal lifetime-activity portfolio and learner-led inclusive activity experience. |
| Ready for Life | 36 | 6 | weekly | A multi-step contribution project with permission, safety, execution, handoff, and reflection evidence. |
| Technology and Computer Science | 36 | 6 | weekly | An accessible human-centered digital product with source, test, privacy, and impact documentation. |
| Arts and Music | 72 | 6 | twice weekly | A curated cross-arts portfolio, performance, exhibition, or private digital gallery. |
| Financial Literacy | 36 | 6 | weekly or twice weekly | A one-year fictional financial plan and oral or written defense; no real account data or individualized financial advice. |

## Evidence and mastery

Progress is based on knowledge, reasoning, application, creation, and retrieval over time. Time spent alone is not mastery. A learner may receive reteaching, a different representation, fewer items, a break, or an alternate response format while the academic target remains intact.

## Family use

- Use `daily-schedule.csv` for a ready-to-run 180-day calendar.
- Use `family-weekly-overview.md` for unit-level planning.
- Open each course folder for the teacher guide, unit plan, assessments, JSONL lesson records, and readable lesson sequence.
- Preserve private learner reflections and do not require photographs, voice recordings, precise location, or account credentials.
