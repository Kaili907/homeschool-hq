# Grade 7 Mathematics — Course Guide

**Course ID:** `ma-g7-mathematics`  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Instructional sessions:** 180  
**Typical session:** 45–60  
**Cadence:** daily

## Course description

A complete Grade 7 mathematics course centered on proportional reasoning, rational numbers, algebra, geometry, statistics, probability, modeling, and explanation.

## Course outcomes

By the end of the course, learners will:

1. Demonstrate the listed Michigan-aligned knowledge and practices through independent, accessible evidence.
2. Explain reasoning, cite evidence, document process, or justify design choices rather than supply unsupported answers.
3. Transfer learning to an unfamiliar task, text, situation, dataset, performance, or project.
4. Use feedback and error evidence to revise without shame or character judgments.
5. Complete the course capstone: **A multi-domain modeling capstone with calculations, representations, limitations, and oral or written defense.**

## Instructional model

Each unit follows a recurring arc: low-stakes launch and diagnostic; explicit modeling; guided practice; independent application; investigation or close reading; performance-task planning and creation; synthesis; unit assessment; targeted correction; and reflection or publication. Lessons are resumable by segment, media is optional, and a readable fallback is always specified.

## Mastery and grading

- One correct answer never establishes mastery.
- Unit assessments combine concept, representation/source use, application, error or claim analysis, connection, performance evidence, and reflection.
- Suggested reporting: **Secure**, **Developing**, or **Not Yet**, supported by evidence rather than a single percentage.
- Reassessment uses fresh items or a new application after targeted instruction.
- Approved breaks, accommodations, voice/no-voice choices, and alternate response modes are not failures.
- Parent summaries show target, completion, evidence type, and next step; they exclude raw private reflections, diagnosis language, and unnecessary personal data.

## Scope and sequence

| Unit | Title | Days | Standards anchors | Performance task |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Mathematical Reasoning and Proportional Thinking | 18 | 7.RP.1, 7.RP.2, MP.1, MP.3, MP.6 | Create a proportional-comparison guide using prices, speeds, recipes, or scale data. |
| 2 | Operations with Rational Numbers | 18 | 7.NS.1, 7.NS.2, 7.NS.3 | Model gains, losses, elevations, temperatures, or account simulations and defend each operation. |
| 3 | Expressions and Equivalent Forms | 18 | 7.EE.1, 7.EE.2 | Create an expression museum showing multiple equivalent forms and the situations each makes easier to analyze. |
| 4 | Equations and Inequalities | 18 | 7.EE.3, 7.EE.4 | Develop a decision model with equations and inequalities for a budget, event, or design constraint. |
| 5 | Percent and Proportional Applications | 18 | 7.RP.3, 7.EE.3 | Audit a fictional shopping, fundraising, or savings plan and explain every percent calculation and assumption. |
| 6 | Scale Drawings and Geometric Constructions | 18 | 7.G.1, 7.G.2 | Create and revise a scale drawing for a room, garden, route, or exhibit. |
| 7 | Circles, Angles, Area, and Surface Area | 18 | 7.G.4, 7.G.5, 7.G.6 | Design a circular or composite structure and provide a complete geometry specification. |
| 8 | Sampling and Statistical Inference | 18 | 7.SP.1, 7.SP.2, 7.SP.3, 7.SP.4 | Conduct a privacy-safe survey or use an approved dataset to compare two groups and state justified limits. |
| 9 | Probability and Simulation | 18 | 7.SP.5, 7.SP.6, 7.SP.7, 7.SP.8 | Design and analyze a fair game or risk simulation, then revise it based on evidence. |
| 10 | Integrated Modeling and Capstone | 18 | 7.RP.3, 7.NS.3, 7.EE.4, 7.G.6, 7.SP.4, MP.4 | Solve a community, household, sport, travel, or design problem using multiple Grade 7 domains. |
