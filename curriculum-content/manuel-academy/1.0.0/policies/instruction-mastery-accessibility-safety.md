# Manuel Academy Curriculum Policy — Instruction, Mastery, Accessibility, Safety, and Privacy

## Instructional commitments

1. Teach for understanding before speed.
2. Use explicit modeling, guided practice, independent application, transfer, and later retrieval.
3. Treat errors as evidence for the next instructional move, never as proof of laziness, character, diagnosis, or worth.
4. Ask one clear action at a time when a learner needs reduced cognitive load.
5. Keep media optional; every lesson must work through readable text, description, transcript, demonstration, or concrete materials.
6. The tutor may explain, question, model, and give feedback, but it must not complete graded work for the learner.

## Mastery rule

A single answer cannot establish mastery. Use multiple evidence types and, where feasible, at least two occasions separated by time or representation. Guided success is not weighted the same as independent success. Low confidence with correct reasoning calls for reassurance and varied practice, not automatic remediation. A pacing concern is not a mastery failure.

## Accessibility

Provide keyboard access, captions, transcripts, alt text, high contrast, reduced motion, readable print, text-only fallbacks, chunked directions, worked examples, extended time, hidden timers, movement breaks, low-distraction settings, and alternate response modes. Preserve the learning target while adjusting quantity, pacing, representation, or response mode.

## Privacy

Do not require identifiable learner photos, raw voice storage, precise location, diagnosis, medical history, family income, faith disclosure, immigration status, real passwords, account credentials, card numbers, tax identifiers, or private messages. Parent-facing summaries show targets, completion, evidence type, and next step—not raw answers or private reflections.

## Ready for Life and health

Use no-shame language. Cleanliness is not personal worth. Nutrition emphasizes variety, adequacy, energy, growth, and recovery. Prohibited practices include calorie targets, dieting, weight cutting, weigh-ins, body-size scoring, earned-food framing, and food morality. Guardian visibility is required for heat, sharp tools, appliances, chemicals, raw-food hazards, medication, transportation, unfamiliar tools, or other safety-critical activities.

## Physical education

Assess safe participation, skill cues, strategy, knowledge, progress, leadership, and reflection. Do not grade body size, body weight, weight change, comparison with peers, or speed alone. Stop for pain, dizziness, breathing difficulty, head impact, or unsafe conditions.

## Sensitive history and literature

Teach enslavement, colonization, war, oppression, discrimination, belief, identity, and trauma with accuracy, context, dignity, and age-appropriate detail. Do not require identity role-play, reenactment of oppression, autobiographical disclosure, or defense of injustice. Offer private or alternate texts and response modes when appropriate.

## Technology and AI

Use approved tools and follow access controls, licensing, and terms. Do not teach bypassing filters or probing real systems. AI output is a draft to verify. Learners must disclose meaningful AI assistance, cite sources, preserve their own reasoning, and never submit AI-completed graded work as their own.

## Financial education

Use de-identified simulations. Do not request real financial credentials or present course content as individualized legal, tax, credit, insurance, or investment advice. Grade 8 personal finance covers Michigan PF1–PF7 content. Michigan guidance allows middle-school coursework to earn the required half credit when the high-school expectations are presented and the learner demonstrates proficiency; importing this package alone does not prove or award that credit.
