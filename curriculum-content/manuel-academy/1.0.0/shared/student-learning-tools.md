# Student Learning Tools

## Explain-your-thinking frame

1. I noticed or knew…
2. I chose…
3. My evidence, model, calculation, quotation, observation, or test was…
4. I checked…
5. I would revise or try next…

## Claim–Evidence–Reasoning frame

- **Claim:** A clear answer to the question.
- **Evidence:** Specific data, source details, observations, examples, or results.
- **Reasoning:** Why the evidence supports the claim using the course concept.
- **Limit:** What the evidence does not show or what remains uncertain.

## Source record

- Creator or organization
- Title
- Date
- Where it was found
- Original source or repost?
- Evidence used
- Reason to trust, question, or limit it
- How it was quoted, paraphrased, or represented

## Project check

- I understand the goal and audience.
- I listed criteria, constraints, materials, and safety needs.
- I made a plan with checkpoints.
- I kept evidence of testing, feedback, or revision.
- I checked accuracy, sources, accessibility, privacy, and presentation.
- I completed the handoff, cleanup, storage, or final “finish the loop” step.
