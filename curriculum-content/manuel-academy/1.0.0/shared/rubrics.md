# Shared Manuel Academy Rubrics

## Four-level mastery rubric

| Level | Knowledge and accuracy | Evidence and reasoning | Application and transfer | Checking and revision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 — Secure and transferable | Accurate and complete | Evidence/reasoning is specific, connected, and convincing | Applies independently in a new context or representation | Checks strategically and improves work substantively |
| 3 — Secure | Mostly accurate with no major misconception | Gives relevant evidence or reasoning | Completes the expected application independently | Uses criteria to correct or clarify |
| 2 — Developing | Partial understanding or inconsistent accuracy | Evidence/reasoning is thin, incomplete, or prompted | Applies with support or only in a familiar form | Notices some issues but needs help revising |
| 1 — Not yet | Prerequisite gap or major misconception is evident | Response is unsupported or not yet interpretable | Cannot yet complete the central application | Needs a different model, smaller step, or new instruction |

A Level 1 or 2 is an instructional signal, not a character judgment. Reassess with fresh evidence after targeted teaching.

## Writing and communication rubric

Score purpose and organization, evidence or development, reasoning or craft, language and conventions, source integrity, and revision. Do not require autobiographical disclosure, public performance, or one specific response mode unless that mode is the standard itself.

## Project and design rubric

Score problem definition, criteria and constraints, planning, safe execution, evidence or testing, iteration, final quality, explanation of tradeoffs, and reflection. A polished appearance cannot replace correct reasoning or safe process.

## Collaboration and discussion rubric

Score preparation, listening, relevant contribution, evidence use, question quality, respectful disagreement, role responsibility, and repair. Do not grade extroversion, eye contact, accent, speech difference, or speed.

## Arts performance/presentation rubric

Score artistic intent, technique, composition or interpretation, rehearsal/process, revision, contextual understanding, attribution, and reflection. Provide private, recorded, small-audience, score-based, or exhibition alternatives when public performance is not required by the target.
