# Standards Reference and Alignment Notes

**Alignment date:** 2026-08-03  
**Jurisdictional focus:** Michigan  
**Status:** Locally authored curriculum aligned to published standards; this package is not a claim of state approval, accreditation, licensure, or automatic credit.

## Alignment approach

- Mathematics and English Language Arts use grade-specific Michigan standards codes.
- Grade 5 science uses Grade 5 performance expectations.
- Grades 7 and 8 science distribute Michigan’s middle-school Grades 6–8 performance-expectation band into a coherent local sequence. The state band does not itself prescribe this exact Grade 7/Grade 8 split.
- Social studies follows Michigan’s Grade 5 U.S. history and geography, Grade 7 world history and geography through approximately 1500 CE, and Grade 8 U.S. history from constitutional foundations through Reconstruction with an introduction to the late nineteenth century.
- Health, physical education, computer science, and the arts use Michigan strand and practice anchors. Local lesson order and activities are curricular decisions.
- Grade 8 Financial Literacy explicitly covers Michigan PF1–PF7. Michigan guidance states that personal finance may be taught in middle school and must count for the half credit when the recommended high-school expectations are presented and proficiency is demonstrated. This package supplies aligned curriculum and assessments, but package possession or import alone does not establish an individual learner’s proficiency or transcript credit.
