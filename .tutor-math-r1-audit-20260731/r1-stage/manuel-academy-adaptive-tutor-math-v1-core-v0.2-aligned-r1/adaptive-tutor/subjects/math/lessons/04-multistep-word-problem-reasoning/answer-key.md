# Multistep Word-Problem Reasoning — Answer Key With Reasoning

> Adult/instructor material. The learner-facing player should reveal these only according to each item’s answer policy.

# Diagnostic answer key

## math-wp-d01 — A school store opens 8 boxes with 6 notebooks in each box. It sells 19 notebooks. Which plan finds how many remain?
**Answer:** B. 8 × 6 − 19
**Reasoning:** First find total notebooks with 8 × 6, then subtract the 19 sold.

## math-wp-d02 — A class has 24 students. Tickets cost $12 per student, and the bus fee is $85. Which expression gives the total trip cost?
**Answer:** B. 24 × 12 + 85
**Reasoning:** Multiply students by cost per student, then add the one-time bus fee.

## math-wp-d03 — A problem says a library has 7 shelves with 24 books each, 38 books are checked out, and the library opened in 1998. Which number is not needed to find books currently on shelves?
**Answer:** D. 1998
**Reasoning:** The opening year does not connect to the quantity of books currently on shelves.

## math-wp-d04 — After calculating 9 boxes × 12 shirts = 108 shirts, what should you ask next if 47 shirts were sold?
**Answer:** B. How many shirts remain after 47 are sold?
**Reasoning:** 108 is an intermediate total; the final question requires subtracting the amount sold.

## math-wp-d05 — Thirty-five tickets cost $8 each, plus a $45 room fee. A group pays $350. Which order is correct for finding change?
**Answer:** B. 35 × 8, then + 45, then subtract from 350
**Reasoning:** Find ticket cost, add the fee, then subtract total cost from amount paid.

## math-wp-d06 — A garden has 6 rows of 14 plants. Twenty-nine plants are given away. Explain how you would solve and check how many remain without calculating the final answer yet.
**Acceptable evidence:**
- Plan: multiply 6 × 14 to find the starting total, then subtract 29.
- Names plants as the unit.
- Check: result must be less than starting total and nonnegative; estimate can be used.
**Reasoning:** A correct plan identifies the dependent intermediate total and a reasonableness check without requiring the final answer immediately.

# Guided-practice answer key

## math-wp-g01 — A club fills 12 bags with 15 pencils each and gives away 68 pencils. Which two operations are needed?
**Answer:** B. Multiplication then subtraction
**Reasoning:** Find total pencils, then remove the amount given away.

## math-wp-g02 — For the pencil problem, write the hidden first question and the final question.
**Acceptable evidence:**
- Hidden: How many pencils are in 12 bags?
- Final: How many pencils remain after 68 are given away?
**Reasoning:** Naming intermediate and final questions prevents stopping early.

## math-wp-g03 — A family buys 4 museum tickets at $18 each and pays a $6 parking fee. What is the total cost?
**Answer:** B. $78
**Reasoning:** 4 × $18 = $72, then $72 + $6 = $78.

## math-wp-g04 — A tank holds 250 liters. It contains 138 liters, then 47 more liters are added. How many more liters are needed to fill it?
**Answer:** A. 65 liters
**Reasoning:** First find 138 + 47 = 185 liters, then 250 − 185 = 65 liters.

## math-wp-g05 — A story includes 5 tables, 8 chairs per table, 3 windows, and 7 extra chairs. Identify the data needed to find the total number of chairs.
**Acceptable evidence:**
- Uses 5 tables, 8 chairs per table, and 7 extra chairs.
- Excludes 3 windows.
- Plans 5 × 8 + 7.
**Reasoning:** The window count is unrelated to the requested chair total.

## math-wp-g06 — Solve and check: A camp orders 9 cases of 24 water bottles and uses 157 bottles. How many remain?
**Acceptable evidence:**
- 9 × 24 = 216.
- 216 − 157 = 59 bottles.
- Checks 59 + 157 = 216 or uses an estimate.
**Reasoning:** The inverse check confirms the remaining and used amounts return to the starting total.

# Independent-mastery answer key

## math-wp-m01 — A theater has 14 rows with 18 seats in each row. Twenty-seven seats are blocked. How many seats are available?
**Answer:** A. 225
**Reasoning:** 14 × 18 = 252, then 252 − 27 = 225.

## math-wp-m02 — A team buys 16 meals at $9 each and pays a $24 delivery fee. It pays with $200. How much change should it receive?
**Answer:** A. $32
**Reasoning:** 16 × $9 = $144; $144 + $24 = $168; $200 − $168 = $32.

## math-wp-m03 — A warehouse has 11 shelves with 32 boxes each. It ships 145 boxes and receives 28 new boxes. Write and solve a step plan for the current number of boxes.
**Acceptable evidence:**
- 11 × 32 = 352.
- 352 − 145 = 207.
- 207 + 28 = 235 boxes.
- Explains the order and unit.
**Reasoning:** The current inventory begins with shelf total, removes shipped boxes, then adds received boxes.

## math-wp-m04 — A recipe card says 6 trays, 15 muffins per tray, 2 oven racks, and 34 muffins served. Which data is relevant to muffins remaining, and why?
**Acceptable evidence:**
- Uses 6, 15, and 34.
- Excludes 2 oven racks because it does not measure muffins.
- Plans 6 × 15 − 34.
**Reasoning:** Only quantities linked to muffin totals are needed.

## math-wp-m05 — A bus can carry 48 passengers. Three buses are available for 137 passengers. Determine whether there are enough seats and explain your check.
**Acceptable evidence:**
- 3 × 48 = 144 seats.
- 144 − 137 = 7 seats remain.
- Concludes there are enough seats and checks units/reasonableness.
**Reasoning:** The capacity exceeds passengers by 7, so all passengers can ride.

## math-wp-m06 — Create a two-step word problem represented by 7 × 16 − 25. Label the units and explain what each step finds.
**Acceptable evidence:**
- Creates a coherent equal-groups context.
- First step finds total 112 units.
- Second step removes 25 and finds 87 units.
- Question asks for the final remaining amount.
**Reasoning:** A valid story matches both operation order and units.
