The canonical external registry remains the immutable 17-slot Session 16 registry. Session 13 supplies the nine durable academic/persistence handles. Session 17 operational composition must supply the recipient resolver, adult-notification permission, durable in-app provider, receipt validator, durable limiter, monitoring, and the outbox handle whose readiness aggregates worker credential verification, scheduling/claim, lease, immutable attempt storage, and operational readiness.

All handles and the registry are frozen and provenance-branded. Production rejects test, fixture, preview, noop, unbranded, or in-memory implementations. Server-side authorization is still mandatory for every method.
